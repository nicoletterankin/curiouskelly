Curious Kelly is built on the belief that curiosity knows no boundaries. We're committed to making world-class education accessible, inclusive, and joyful for all.

Our Mission

Education should be a universal right, not a privilege. Curious Kelly exists to democratize access to personalized, adaptive learning for every human—regardless of age, background, ability, language, or circumstance.

We design for the 2-year-old discovering shapes, the 102-year-old exploring astronomy, the neurodivergent learner who needs flexible pacing, the rural family with limited resources, and everyone in between.

Accessibility Commitments

We are actively working to meet and exceed WCAG 2.1 AA standards. Accessibility is not an afterthought—it's a core design principle.

🎧
Audio-First Design
Every lesson is fully narrated by Kelly. Learners with visual impairments or reading difficulties can learn through listening.
📝
Full Transcripts & Captions
All audio includes synchronized transcripts and captions. Deaf and hard-of-hearing learners have equal access.
⚡
Adjustable Speed & Pace
Control playback speed (0.5x to 2x). Pause, rewind, and replay as many times as needed. No pressure, no timers.
🎨
High Contrast & Large Text
Dark mode by default with high contrast ratios. Text scales to your preference. Dyslexia-friendly fonts available.
⌨️
Keyboard Navigation
Full keyboard support for users who cannot use a mouse. Screen reader compatible with ARIA labels.
🌐
Multilingual Support
Lessons available in English, Spanish, and French. More languages coming soon. All content precomputed for quality.

Neurodiversity & Learning Differences

Curious Kelly is designed for neurodivergent learners, including those with ADHD, autism, dyslexia, and other learning differences.

How We Support Diverse Learners

  • No Forced Pacing: Learn at your own speed. Pause mid-lesson and resume anytime.
  • Predictable Structure: Every lesson follows a consistent format to reduce cognitive load.
  • Sensory Considerations: Minimal animations, no flashing content, calming color palette.
  • Choice and Agency: Learners can skip, replay, or explore related topics freely.
  • Positive Reinforcement: Focus on progress and curiosity, not grades or competition.
  • Quiet Focus Mode: Disable notifications and distractions during lessons.

Economic Accessibility

We believe cost should never be a barrier to education. While we charge $199/year to sustain our mission, we are committed to expanding access.

Current Initiatives

  • Gift Subscriptions: Sponsor a learner in need through our gift program
  • Library Partnerships: Free access through participating public libraries (coming 2026)
  • Scholarship Program: Need-based scholarships for families (launching Q1 2026)
  • Affiliate Program: Earn by sharing Curious Kelly with your community

Future Goals

  • Partner with Title I schools to provide free access to underserved students
  • Offer sliding-scale pricing based on household income
  • Create a "1-for-1" model: every paid subscription funds a free one

Cultural Representation

Our curriculum celebrates diverse cultures, perspectives, and histories. We are intentional about whose stories we tell and how we tell them.

Curriculum Principles

  • Global Perspectives: Science, history, and art from all continents and cultures
  • Inclusive Language: Gender-neutral examples, diverse names and scenarios
  • Authentic Voices: Consult experts and community members for cultural topics
  • Avoid Stereotypes: Rigorous review process to eliminate bias and tokenism

Team Diversity (Our Commitment)

As a small, early-stage team, we are committed to building a diverse, equitable, and inclusive organization as we grow.

Our Goals

  • Recruit from underrepresented communities in tech and education
  • Pay equitable salaries with transparent compensation bands
  • Offer flexible work arrangements for caregivers and people with disabilities
  • Provide professional development and mentorship opportunities
  • Measure and report on diversity metrics annually

We acknowledge we have work to do. Diversity is not a checkbox—it's an ongoing practice.
