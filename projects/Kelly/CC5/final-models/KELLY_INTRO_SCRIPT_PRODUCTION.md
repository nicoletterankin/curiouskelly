## THE SCRIPT (CANONICAL)

```
Did you know that octopuses have three hearts? Two pump blood to the gills. One pumps it to the body. And when they swim? That third heart stops.

I learned that yesterday. And it made me think — if an octopus can literally stop its heart to move forward... what's my excuse?

That's what I do. I find one thing every day that makes me see the world differently. And I share it with you.

Five minutes. One idea. Every single day.

Do that for a year? The world gets a lot more interesting. And so do you.

I'm Kelly.

Ready?
```

---

## WHY THIS WORKS — The Transformation Pattern

This script IS the pattern that every lesson must follow:

| Step | Line | Purpose |
|------|------|---------|
| **HOOK** | "Octopuses have three hearts..." | Unexpected fact that stops them |
| **BRIDGE** | "I learned that yesterday..." | Kelly's personal curiosity (WITH, not above) |
| **MIRROR** | "...what's my excuse?" | Connects to THEM — "what does this mean for ME?" |
| **SHIFT** | "See the world differently" | Not trivia — transformation |
| **ACCUMULATION** | "Do that for a year? The world gets more interesting. And so do you." | The compound promise — 365 days of this |
| **INVITATION** | "Ready?" | Not demand — choice |

**This pattern must be in EVERY lesson. See `docs/brand/KELLY_TRANSFORMATION_PATTERN.md`**
