# Year 2: AI Fluency & Meta-Learning
## 365 Days of Learning How to Learn in the Age of AI

**Status:** Complete Curriculum Roadmap
**Launch Target:** School Year 2026-2027
**Prerequisites:** None (but designed as Year 2 after Year 1: Foundations)

---

## Executive Summary

Year 2 of Curious Kelly teaches **how to learn in the age of AI**—not AI itself, but the meta-skills, critical thinking, and human capabilities that matter more than ever when AI is ubiquitous. This isn't a tech curriculum; it's a **human empowerment curriculum** for an AI-enhanced world.

### Why This Matters for Teachers

> *"AI can deliver information, but only humans can inspire learning."*

Teachers are burned out delivering content that AI can now deliver. Year 2 repositions teachers as:
- **Learning coaches** (not information dispensers)
- **Critical thinking guides** (verifying what AI generates)
- **Human connection facilitators** (what AI can never do)
- **Curriculum curators** (choosing from pre-built lessons)

**Teacher Earnings Maximization:** Every lesson is pre-built, pre-tested, and pre-optimized. Teachers can focus on what they do best: human connection, personalization, and inspiration.

## Daily Topic Distribution

Each month has ~30 lessons following this pattern:

### Week 1: Core Concepts (Days 1-7)
Foundational ideas that establish the month's theme

### Week 2: Skills & Practice (Days 8-14)
Hands-on application and skill building

### Week 3: Deep Dives (Days 15-21)
Exploring nuances, edge cases, advanced thinking

### Week 4: Integration (Days 22-28/30/31)
Connecting to real life, other subjects, previous months

## Teacher Integration

### For Classroom Use

Each lesson includes:
- **Teacher Guide:** 5-minute pre-class prep
- **Discussion Prompts:** Age-appropriate questions
- **Extension Activities:** Optional deeper dives
- **Assessment Rubric:** What good looks like
- **Cross-Curricular Links:** Connections to other subjects

### Scheduling Options

| Model | Description | Teacher Time |
|-------|-------------|--------------|
| **Daily Integration** | 8-10 min per day | 5 min prep |
| **Weekly Block** | 40-50 min once weekly | 15 min prep |
| **Monthly Module** | Half-day deep dive | 30 min prep |
| **Self-Paced** | Student-driven, teacher check-ins | As needed |

### Teacher Earnings

- All lessons pre-built = zero content creation time
- Teacher guides pre-written = minimal prep
- Assessment rubrics included = streamlined grading
- Focus shifts to facilitation = higher impact, less burnout
