Children's Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 27, 2026

Designed for Children's Safety

Curious Kelly is designed for learners of all ages, including children under 13. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and take children's privacy seriously.

Parent Summary:

  • No personal data required - Children can learn anonymously
  • No advertising - We never show ads or sell data
  • Parental consent - Required before collecting any personal information
  • You're in control - Review, download, or delete your child's data anytime

1. Our Commitment to Children's Privacy

At Lesson of the Day PBC ("Curious Kelly," "we," "us"), we believe every child deserves safe, high-quality educational content. We are committed to protecting children's privacy and complying with all applicable laws, including:

  • COPPA - Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (United States)
  • GDPR-K - General Data Protection Regulation provisions for children (European Union)
  • Age Appropriate Design Code - UK Information Commissioner's Office standards

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information We Collect From Children

We minimize data collection from children. Here's exactly what we collect:

Data Type Collected? Purpose
Name NO Not required
Email address NO Not collected from children
Phone number NO Not collected
Location NO Not collected
Photos/Videos NO Not collected
Lesson progress YES Track which lessons completed (stored locally unless parent creates account)
Anonymous votes YES Thumbs up/down on lessons (no user identification)
Device type YES Ensure app works correctly on device

No Registration Required: Children can use Curious Kelly and learn without creating an account or providing any personal information. Progress is stored locally on the device.

2.2 Information Collected With Verifiable Parental Consent

If a parent creates a Family Account, we may collect additional information to provide enhanced features:

  • Parent's email - For account management and communication (not the child's)
  • Child's display name - A nickname chosen by the parent (no real name required)
  • Age range - To personalize lesson difficulty (e.g., "5-7" or "8-10")
  • Learning progress - Synced across devices for continuity

2.3 Information We Never Collect From Children

  • Real names or full names
  • School name or address
  • Social security numbers or government IDs
  • Photographs, videos, or voice recordings
  • Geolocation or precise location
  • Contact lists or friends lists
  • Financial information
  • Biometric data

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information solely for educational purposes:

  • Deliver lessons - Show age-appropriate educational content
  • Track progress - Remember which lessons have been completed
  • Improve content - Anonymous voting helps us make better lessons
  • Technical support - Fix bugs and ensure the app works correctly

We Never Use Children's Data For:

  • Advertising or marketing
  • Behavioral targeting or profiling
  • Selling to third parties
  • Creating interest profiles
  • Social media integration

4. No Third-Party Advertising

Curious Kelly does not show advertisements. We are funded by subscriptions (paid by parents), not advertising. Children will never see:

  • Banner ads
  • Video ads
  • Sponsored content
  • In-app purchase prompts directed at children
  • Third-party tracking pixels

5. Data Sharing

We Never Sell Children's Data. Ever.

We only share data in these limited circumstances:

5.1 With Parents

Parents with Family Accounts can view their child's progress, completed lessons, and activity.

5.2 Service Providers

We use trusted service providers who have signed strict data protection agreements:

  • Cloudflare - Content delivery and security (no personal data stored)
  • Supabase - Secure database hosting (encrypted, access-controlled)

5.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety of children.

6. Parental Consent (COPPA)

6.1 When Consent Is Required

Under COPPA, we obtain verifiable parental consent before:

  • Collecting any personal information from a child under 13
  • Creating a child's account within a Family Account
  • Syncing a child's progress to the cloud

6.2 How We Verify Parental Consent

We use the following methods to verify parental consent:

  • Credit card verification - A small authorization charge (refunded) through our payment processor
  • Email confirmation + knowledge-based questions - For accounts without payment
  • Government ID verification - Available as an alternative option

6.3 Consent Is Revocable

Parents may withdraw consent at any time by contacting us or using the account settings. We will delete all collected data within 48 hours.

For Parents: Your Rights

As a parent or legal guardian, you have the right to:

  • Review - See all data collected about your child
  • Download - Export your child's data in a portable format
  • Correct - Update or fix any information
  • Delete - Request permanent deletion of all data
  • Refuse - Decline future data collection

To exercise these rights:
Email: hello@curiouskelly.com
Subject: "Parent Data Request"
Response time: Within 48 hours

7. Data Security

We protect children's data with enterprise-grade security:

  • Encryption in transit - All data transmitted over TLS 1.3
  • Encryption at rest - All stored data encrypted with AES-256
  • Access controls - Only essential personnel can access data
  • Regular audits - Security practices reviewed quarterly
  • Incident response - Documented plan for potential breaches

8. Data Retention and Deletion

8.1 Retention Periods

Data Type Retention Period
Anonymous usage data 90 days, then aggregated/anonymized
Lesson progress (no account) Stored locally on device only
Family Account data Until account deletion or 1 year of inactivity
Anonymous votes Permanently (no personal data attached)

8.2 Automatic Deletion

If a Family Account is inactive for 12 months, we will:

  1. Send a reminder email to the parent
  2. Wait 30 days for response
  3. Automatically delete all child data if no response

8.3 Immediate Deletion

Parents can request immediate deletion at any time. We will delete all data within 48 hours, except where retention is required by law.

9. Child Safety Measures

Beyond privacy, we implement these safety features:

  • No social features - Children cannot communicate with other users
  • No user-generated content - Children cannot post, comment, or share
  • No external links - Lessons don't link to external websites
  • Content moderation - All lessons are reviewed for age-appropriateness
  • No push notifications to children - Only parents receive notifications
  • Session time limits - Optional parental controls for screen time

10. Cookies and Tracking

We use minimal, essential cookies only:

  • Session cookies - Required for the app to function (no tracking)
  • Preference cookies - Remember language and display settings

We do NOT use:

  • Third-party tracking cookies
  • Advertising cookies
  • Analytics cookies that identify users
  • Cross-site tracking

11. International Data

Curious Kelly is operated from the United States. If you access our service from outside the US, data may be transferred to the US for processing. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for international transfers, including Standard Contractual Clauses where required.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. For material changes:

  • We will notify parents via email at least 30 days before changes take effect
  • We will obtain new parental consent if required by COPPA
  • We will post a notice on our website

Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance, but we will always seek new consent for children's data when required.

California residents have additional rights under CCPA/CPRA:

15. FTC Compliance

This Privacy Policy complies with the Federal Trade Commission's COPPA Rule (16 CFR Part 312). If you believe we have not complied with COPPA, you may file a complaint with the FTC at ftc.gov/complaint.
