🌟 The Kelly Story

Curious Kelly is built by a solo mom who loves playing with AI and being outside. 730 daily lessons. Zero paywalls. Community-powered.

We don't believe in gating education. Never gate anyone.. ever.. Instead, we invite the internet to contribute their unused resources.

Lesson of the Day, PBC is a California Public Benefit Corporation. Our mission: one meaningful lesson, every day, for life.
