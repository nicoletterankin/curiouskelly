Usage Guidelines

Do

  • Use the Kelly face mark as the primary brand icon
  • Maintain the circular crop for profile and icon uses
  • Use Kelly Blue (#2563eb) as the primary accent color
  • Keep adequate clear space around the logo
  • Use "Curious Kelly" as the product name
  • Use "Lesson of the Day PBC" for legal/corporate references

Don't

  • Don't use emoji in place of Kelly face icons
  • Don't distort, rotate, or alter the Kelly mark
  • Don't place Kelly mark on busy backgrounds without contrast
  • Don't use low-resolution versions of assets
  • Don't create derivative logos or modify Kelly's appearance
