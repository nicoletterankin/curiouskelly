Frequently Asked Questions

How long is the cookie duration?
30 days. If someone clicks your link and subscribes within 30 days, you get credit.
When do I get paid?
Scholar tier: Monthly (minimum $50 balance)
Fellow tier: Bi-weekly
Ambassador tier: Weekly
Payments via PayPal, bank transfer, or check.
What if a subscriber cancels?
You earn commissions only while the subscriber is active. If they cancel, your commission stops. If they re-subscribe within 12 months, you get credit again.
Can I promote to my email list?
Yes! Email, social media, blog posts, videos, podcasts—any channel where you have an authentic audience.
Is there a minimum audience size?
No. We care more about engagement and authenticity than follower count. Micro-influencers often convert better than mega-influencers.
What marketing materials do you provide?
Banners, email templates, social media graphics, demo videos, and sample copy. Fellow and Ambassador tiers get custom landing pages.
How is the Founding 100 offer different?
The first 100 approved affiliates get locked into 30% commission rate forever—even at the Scholar tier. This offer expires December 31, 2025.
