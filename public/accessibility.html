Accessibility Statement

Accessibility

Curious Kelly is committed to making daily learning accessible to everyone. We believe curiosity knows no bounds, and neither should access to knowledge.

Last updated: January 27, 2026

Our Accessibility Commitment

We are committed to conforming to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA. We continuously improve our platform to ensure all learners can access our educational content.

Target: WCAG 2.1 Level AA

Why Accessibility Matters

At Lesson of the Day PBC, we believe education should be available to everyone, regardless of ability. Accessible design benefits all users—not just those with disabilities—by creating clearer, more usable interfaces.

Our accessibility work follows the four principles of WCAG: content must be Perceivable, Operable, Understandable, and Robust.

Curious Kelly includes the following accessibility features:

⌨️

Works on all device sizes, orientations, and zoom levels.

Screen Reader Support

Curious Kelly is designed to work with assistive technologies. We test regularly with the following screen readers:

  • VoiceOver (macOS and iOS) - Primary testing platform
  • NVDA (Windows) - Tested with Firefox and Chrome
  • JAWS (Windows) - Tested with Chrome
  • TalkBack (Android) - Mobile accessibility

ARIA Implementation

We use ARIA (Accessible Rich Internet Applications) attributes to enhance screen reader experience:

  • Proper heading hierarchy (h1-h6)
  • Descriptive link text (no "click here")
  • ARIA labels for interactive elements
  • ARIA live regions for dynamic content updates
  • Role attributes for custom components
  • Focus management in modals and menus

Visual Accessibility

Color Contrast

All text meets WCAG 2.1 AA contrast requirements:

  • Normal text: Minimum 4.5:1 contrast ratio
  • Large text: Minimum 3:1 contrast ratio
  • UI components: Minimum 3:1 contrast ratio

No Color-Only Information

We never rely solely on color to convey information. All status indicators include text labels or icons in addition to color changes.

Reduced Motion

Curious Kelly respects the prefers-reduced-motion system preference. Users who prefer reduced motion will see simplified animations.

Audio and Video Accessibility

  • Audio descriptions: All visual content is described in narration
  • Text transcripts: Full transcripts for all lessons
  • Captions: Closed captions available for video content
  • Volume control: Independent volume controls
  • Playback speed: Adjustable speed (0.5x to 2x)

Conformance Status

The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) define requirements for designers and developers to improve accessibility for people with disabilities.

Current status: Curious Kelly partially conforms to WCAG 2.1 Level AA. We are actively working toward full conformance.

Known Limitations

Despite our best efforts, some areas may have accessibility limitations:

  • Kelly's animated avatar: The animated character's expressions and gestures are described in narration but may not be fully conveyed to screen reader users.
  • Interactive infographics: Some visual learning materials are being converted to accessible formats. Text alternatives are available.
  • Third-party embeds: Some embedded content from third parties may not meet our accessibility standards.

If you encounter any accessibility barriers, please contact us for alternative formats or assistance.

Testing Methodology

We assess accessibility through:

  • Automated testing: axe-core, WAVE, Lighthouse
  • Manual testing: Keyboard navigation, screen reader testing
  • User testing: Regular feedback from users with disabilities
  • Third-party audits: Periodic professional accessibility audits
