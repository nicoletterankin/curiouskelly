## Public Benefit Corporation Status

### What is a PBC?

A Public Benefit Corporation is a for-profit entity that is legally required to consider:
1. Shareholder value
2. Stakeholder impact (employees, community)
3. Public benefit (stated mission)

### Our Public Benefit Statement

> *"To make world-class education accessible to everyone, regardless of geography, income, or native language, through innovative AI-powered teaching technology."*

### Why PBC Structure?

| Benefit | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Mission Protection | Directors legally must consider educational impact |
| Investor Alignment | Attracts mission-aligned capital |
| Employee Attraction | Purpose-driven talent prefers PBCs |
| Exit Flexibility | Can still be acquired (Patagonia, Kickstarter precedent) |
