# Nicolette Kefaya — Founder & CEO

## The 20-Year EdTech Journey

Nicolette Kefaya has spent two decades at the intersection of education and technology, driven by a singular mission: **make world-class education accessible to everyone, regardless of geography, income, or native language.**

---

## Career Highlights

### Open English (2007-2015)
**Role:** Founding Team Member, VP of Curriculum

- Built the curriculum engine for what became Latin America's largest online English school
- Scaled from 0 to 400,000+ paying students
- Pioneered live video tutoring at scale before Zoom existed
- Developed the "micro-lesson" methodology that achieved 3x retention vs traditional courses
- Company reached $100M+ annual revenue

**Key Learning:** Students don't fail because content is bad—they fail because habit formation is broken.

---

### Independent EdTech Consultant (2015-2020)
**Clients:** Pearson, McGraw-Hill, Rosetta Stone, various startups

- Advised on AI integration strategies before GPT existed
- Designed adaptive learning systems for 5 major publishers
- Identified the "personalization paradox"—most adaptive systems make learning *harder*, not easier

**Key Learning:** Technology should reduce cognitive load, not add features.

---

### Curious Kelly / The Daily Lesson (2020-Present)
**Role:** Founder & CEO

- Conceived and built Kelly—the world's first AI character designed specifically to teach
- Developed the "Phase DNA" curriculum system enabling infinite content generation
- Built voice training pipeline producing studio-quality synthetic speech
- Architected the local-first inference system for data privacy

---

## Philosophy

> "Duolingo gamified language learning. We're *humanizing* it. Kelly isn't a chatbot or a game—she's your tireless, infinitely patient teacher who remembers everything you've learned and adapts to exactly what you need next."
