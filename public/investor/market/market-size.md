## Market Timing

### Why Now?

```
2015-2019: AI too primitive
           └── Chatbots were frustrating
           └── Voice synthesis was robotic
           └── No personalization possible

2020-2022: Pandemic acceleration
           └── Online learning normalized
           └── Screen fatigue emerged
           └── Demand for human connection

2023-2025: AI inflection point ← WE ARE HERE
```
