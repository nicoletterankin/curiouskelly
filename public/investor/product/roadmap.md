## 2030: Strategic Exit

### Acquisition Targets

| Acquirer | Strategic Fit | Probability |
|----------|--------------|-------------|
| **Apple** | Education initiative, services growth | High |
| **Google** | YouTube Learning, Android default | Medium |
| **Microsoft** | LinkedIn Learning, enterprise | Medium |
| **Duolingo** | Technology acquisition | Medium |
| **Pearson** | Digital transformation | Low |

### Exit Valuation Drivers

| Factor | Impact |
|--------|--------|
| Subscriber count | High |
| Revenue multiple | High |
| Technology (voice, video) | Medium |
| Curriculum library | Medium |
| Team expertise | Medium |

### Apple Acquisition Thesis

**Why Apple Wants Kelly:**

1. **Services Revenue** — Subscription business aligns with Apple's growth strategy
2. **Education Market** — iPad in schools needs better software
3. **AI Differentiation** — Character AI vs. generic Siri
4. **Privacy Story** — Local inference matches Apple's brand
5. **Content Moat** — Curriculum library is defensible

**Valuation Range:** $500M - $2B (depending on metrics)
