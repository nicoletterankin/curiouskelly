# Product Overview

## What is Curious Kelly?

**Curious Kelly is the world's first AI teacher designed to build daily learning habits for lifelong learners.**

Unlike chatbots (too open-ended) or apps like Duolingo (too game-like), Kelly is a *character*—a patient, encouraging teacher who delivers one lesson per day, every day, forever.

---

## The Core Insight

> **People don't fail at learning because content is bad. They fail because habit formation is broken.**

Every competitor focuses on content volume. We focus on *daily completion*. One lesson per day × 365 days = transformation.

---

## How It Works

### The Daily Lesson Experience

```
1. WAKE UP → Kelly notification: "Good morning! Ready for today's lesson?"

2. OPEN APP → Kelly greets you by name, references yesterday's topic

3. 5 MINUTES → Interactive lesson with voice, visuals, questions
   • Kelly speaks every word (no reading walls of text)
   • 2-3 comprehension checks built in
   • Ends with "wisdom moment" synthesis

4. COMPLETE → Progress updates, streak continues

5. TOMORROW → Kelly returns with the next topic, building on today
```

### Kelly's Superpowers

| Capability | How It Works |
|-----------|--------------|
| **Remembers Everything** | Your history, preferences, struggles |
| **Adapts to You** | Harder questions if you're crushing it |
| **Never Judges** | Wrong answers lead to patient re-teaching |
| **Always Available** | 6 AM or midnight, Kelly's ready |
| **Infinitely Patient** | Ask her to repeat anything, anytime |

## The Product Stack

### Layer 1: Kelly (The Character)

- **Voice:** Custom-trained ElevenLabs model, warm and encouraging
- **Appearance:** Consistent visual identity across all content
- **Personality:** Curious, patient, celebrates progress
- **Memory:** Knows your entire learning history

### Layer 2: Curriculum Engine (Phase DNA)

Every lesson follows the same proven structure:

```
PHASE 0: Welcome (0:30)
├── Greeting by name
├── Reference to previous lesson
└── Today's topic intro

PHASES 1-4: Core Teaching (3:00)
├── Concept introduction
├── Real-world example
├── Comprehension check
└── Adaptive follow-up

PHASE 5: Wisdom (1:00)
├── Key takeaway
├── Connection to bigger picture
└── Preview of tomorrow
```

### Layer 3: Content Library

- **365 lessons** per topic track
- **Pre-written by educators**, not AI-generated on-the-fly
- **Multi-language support** (EN, ES, FR)
- **Expandable to any subject**
