How Kelly Addresses Each SDG 4 Target

Every target in SDG 4 has a Kelly implementation. Here's how we're building infrastructure for the world's most ambitious education goal.

4.1
Universal Primary & Secondary

By 2030, ensure all girls and boys complete free, equitable and quality primary and secondary education.

Kelly's Answer
Age-adaptive curriculum for ages 13+, available 365 days/year
4.2
Early Childhood Development

Access to quality early childhood development, care and pre-primary education.

Kelly's Answer
Teen-friendly content with engaging, voice-enabled design
4.3
Equal Access to Affordable Education

Equal access for all to affordable and quality technical, vocational and tertiary education.

Kelly's Answer
$199/year + scholarship fund for those who can't afford
4.4
Skills for Employment

Increase the number of youth and adults who have relevant skills for employment.

Kelly's Answer
Practical life skills curriculum including technology, finance, communication
4.5
Eliminate Gender Disparities

Eliminate gender disparities in education and ensure equal access for the vulnerable.

Kelly's Answer
Equal access design, inclusive content, scholarship priority for underserved
4.6
Universal Literacy

Ensure all youth and a substantial proportion of adults achieve literacy and numeracy.

Kelly's Answer
Voice-first design accessible to pre-literate learners of all ages
4.7
Education for Sustainable Development

Ensure learners acquire knowledge and skills needed for sustainable development.

Kelly's Answer
Citizenship, empathy, global thinking, environmental stewardship curriculum
