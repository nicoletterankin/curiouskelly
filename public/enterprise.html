How one school district uses Kelly to engage students.

Pilot Program

Oakwood Elementary School District

Oakwood Elementary piloted Curious Kelly with 250 students across grades 3-5 during the 2025 spring semester. The district integrated Kelly into their enrichment program, offering 15-minute daily sessions during independent study time.

Teachers reported increased student engagement, particularly among learners who struggled with traditional instruction. The adaptive pacing and voice-first design proved especially effective for ESL students and those with reading difficulties.
