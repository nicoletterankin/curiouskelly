✨ Trust & Safety

Honest by Design

Social media hijacked social learning. We're taking it back—ethically. Here's how Kelly creates safe, transparent social learning experiences.

🧠 Why Social Learning Matters

Humans are inherently social learners. We watch others, we ask questions together, we feel belonging. This isn't optional—it's how we evolved to learn.

Social media exploited this need by creating addictive, unpredictable feedback loops. Kelly offers something different: predictable, safe, growth-oriented social mirrors.

❌ Social Media

  • Hidden algorithms
  • Variable rewards (addiction)
  • Engagement optimization
  • No user control
  • Status competition
  • Deceptive (bots as real)

✓ Curious Kelly

  • Transparent simulation
  • Predictable content

  • Full user control
  • Growth mindset focus
  • Every comment marked ✨

✨ What We Simulate

During lessons, you may see comments from simulated learners. These are AI-generated to create a supportive social learning experience.

Simulated comments are designed to:

  • Normalize asking questions and feeling confused
  • Show diverse perspectives from around the world
  • Create a sense of shared learning experience
  • Model growth mindset (struggle is normal!)

🎛️ You're in Control

📜 Our Principles

1

Transparency Over Deception

Every piece of simulated content is marked. We never hide that it's AI-generated.

2

Predictability Over Variable Rewards

No algorithmic optimization for engagement. No addiction mechanics.

3

Growth Mindset Over Status

Simulated comments model struggle and learning, not perfection.

4

Control Over Coercion

Turn it off with one toggle. No dark patterns, no guilt.

5

Education Over Engagement

Every feature exists to help learning—never to increase time-on-app.

6

Safety Over Speed

We'll delay features rather than ship something that could cause harm.

🚫 What We'll Never Do

🛑 Red Lines

  • Claim simulated users are real people
  • Use simulated content to manipulate emotions
  • Add variable rewards or addiction mechanics
  • Hide disclosure indicators
  • Make it hard to turn off
  • Use simulated content to sell or upsell
  • Exploit loneliness or need for connection
