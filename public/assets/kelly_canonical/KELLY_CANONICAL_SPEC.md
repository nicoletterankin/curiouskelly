## ⚠️ COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

### Hair
- ❌ Making hair too dark (black) or too light (blonde)
- ❌ Tight curls instead of soft waves
- ❌ Straight hair with no wave
- ❌ Side part instead of center part
- ❌ Hair too short or too long

### Sweater
- ❌ **RIBBED TEXTURE** — Must be soft cashmere
- ❌ Wrong shade of blue (teal, navy, purple)
- ❌ Wrong garment type (cardigan, hoodie, t-shirt)
- ❌ Visible knit pattern

### Face
- ❌ Wrong age (too young/old)
- ❌ Wrong eye color
- ❌ Unnatural expressions
- ❌ Inconsistent facial features

## 🔧 TECHNICAL SPECS FOR VFX/3D

### Hair System
- **Strand Count**: ~100,000 guide strands
- **Wave Frequency**: Low-medium (beach wave pattern)
- **Highlight Distribution**: 20-30% of strands, concentrated at crown/face-frame
- **Physics**: Natural gravity fall, slight bounce

### Material Properties
- **Hair Specularity**: 0.3-0.4 (healthy shine)
- **Hair Roughness**: 0.4-0.5
- **Sweater Roughness**: 0.7-0.8 (soft matte cashmere)
- **Sweater Fuzz**: Enable subsurface fuzz for cashmere effect

### Lighting Reference
- **Key Light**: Soft from 45° front-left, slightly above eye level
- **Fill Light**: Gentle from 45° front-right, lower intensity
- **Rim Light**: Subtle hair edge separation
