🚀 Getting Started

What is Curious Kelly? ▼
Curious Kelly is an AI teacher that delivers one fascinating lesson every day. Each lesson takes about 5 minutes and adapts to each learner—mood, language, and pace. Our mission is to make lifelong learning accessible to everyone.
How does it work? ▼
Every day, Kelly presents a new topic with an interactive lesson. Learners engage through questions, explanations, and discoveries. At the end, Kelly shares a "wisdom" – a key insight to remember. Lessons are designed to spark curiosity and make learning enjoyable.
What ages is this for? ▼
Curious Kelly is designed for learners ages 13 and up. The content adapts to different age groups – from teenagers to seniors. The age slider adjusts content accordingly. Note: Learners must be 13 or older to create an account.
How do I get started? ▼
Today's lesson is always yours—no signup needed, no credit card, ever. To unlock the full archive of 365 lessons and learn at your own pace, a subscription is required.
What devices can I use? ▼
Curious Kelly works on any device with a modern web browser – phones, tablets, laptops, and desktops. We recommend Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge. Native iOS and Android apps are coming soon!

👤 Account & Login

How do I create an account? ▼
Click "Start Learning" on the homepage. Sign up with Google, Apple, or an email address. Learners must confirm they are 13 or older to create an account.
I forgot my password ▼
Click "Sign In" then "Forgot Password." Enter the email address and a reset link will be sent. Check the spam folder if the email doesn't appear. For Google or Apple signups, use those buttons to log in instead.
I'm not receiving emails ▼
Check the spam/junk folder first. Add hello@curiouskelly.com to contacts to ensure delivery. If issues persist, try a different email address or sign up with Google/Apple instead.
How do I delete my account? ▼
Email us at hello@curiouskelly.com with the subject "Delete Account" and we'll process the request within 48 hours. This will permanently delete the account and all associated data.
Can my child under 13 use Curious Kelly? ▼
Currently, users must be 13 or older to create an account. We're developing family accounts that will allow parents to create supervised accounts for younger children. Email us at hello@curiouskelly.com to join the waitlist for family features.

🔧 Technical Issues

Something isn't working ▼
Try these steps: 1) Refresh the page, 2) Clear the browser cache, 3) Try a different browser, 4) Check the internet connection. If it's still not working, email us at hello@curiouskelly.com with details about the issue.
The site is loading slowly ▼
Slow loading is usually due to the internet connection. Try: 1) Moving closer to the WiFi router, 2) Closing other browser tabs, 3) Using a wired connection if possible. If the problem persists, let us know.
I can't hear Kelly's voice ▼
Make sure the device volume is up and not muted. Check that the browser has permission to play audio (look for a speaker icon in the address bar). Some browsers block autoplay audio – a click may be needed to start.
