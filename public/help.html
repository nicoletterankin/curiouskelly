🚀 Getting Started

What is Curious Kelly? ▼
Curious Kelly is your personal AI teacher that delivers one fascinating lesson every day. Each lesson takes about 5 minutes and adapts to your age and interests. Our mission is to make lifelong learning accessible to everyone.
How does it work? ▼
Every day, Kelly presents a new topic with an interactive lesson. You'll learn through questions, explanations, and discoveries. At the end, Kelly shares a "wisdom" – a key insight to remember. Lessons are designed to spark curiosity and make learning enjoyable.
What ages is this for? ▼
Curious Kelly is designed for learners ages 13 and up. Our content adapts to different age groups – from teenagers to seniors. Use the age slider to get content that's right for you. Note: Users must be 13 or older to create an account.
Is it free? ▼
You can access today's lesson for free every day! To unlock the full archive of 365 lessons and learn at your own pace, you'll need a subscription. We offer a 7-day free trial so you can explore everything before committing.
What devices can I use? ▼
Curious Kelly works on any device with a modern web browser – phones, tablets, laptops, and desktops. We recommend Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge. Native iOS and Android apps are coming soon!

👤 Account & Login

How do I create an account? ▼
Click "Start Free" on our homepage. You can sign up with Google, Apple, or your email address. You'll need to confirm you're 13 or older to create an account.
I forgot my password ▼
Click "Sign In" then "Forgot Password." Enter your email address and we'll send you a reset link. Check your spam folder if you don't see it. If you signed up with Google or Apple, use those buttons to log in instead.
I'm not receiving emails ▼
Check your spam/junk folder first. Add hello@curiouskelly.com to your contacts to ensure delivery. If you still have issues, try using a different email address or sign up with Google/Apple instead.
How do I delete my account? ▼
Email us at hello@curiouskelly.com with the subject "Delete Account" and we'll process your request within 48 hours. This will permanently delete your account and all associated data.
Can my child under 13 use Curious Kelly? ▼
Currently, users must be 13 or older to create an account. We're developing family accounts that will allow parents to create supervised accounts for younger children. Email us at hello@curiouskelly.com to join the waitlist for family features.

💳 Billing & Plans

What plans do you offer? ▼
We offer Monthly ($9.99/mo), Annual ($99/year - 2 months free), and Lifetime ($299 one-time). All plans include full access to every lesson, all languages, and family sharing. Visit curiouskelly.com/pricing for details.
How do I cancel my subscription? ▼
You can cancel anytime by emailing hello@curiouskelly.com. Your access continues until the end of your current billing period. We don't believe in making cancellation difficult.
What's your refund policy? ▼
We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied within your first 7 days, email us for a full refund, no questions asked. After 7 days, we don't offer refunds but you can cancel anytime to prevent future charges.
Can I gift a subscription? ▼
Yes! Visit curiouskelly.com/gifts to purchase a gift subscription. You'll receive a gift code that never expires. The recipient can redeem it at curiouskelly.com/redeem.
Can multiple people use one subscription? ▼
Yes! One subscription works for your whole household. Each family member can adjust the age slider to get age-appropriate content. We trust you to share responsibly within your family.

🔧 Technical Issues

Something isn't working ▼
Try these steps: 1) Refresh the page, 2) Clear your browser cache, 3) Try a different browser, 4) Check your internet connection. If it's still not working, email us at hello@curiouskelly.com with details about what you're experiencing.
The site is loading slowly ▼
Slow loading is usually due to your internet connection. Try: 1) Moving closer to your WiFi router, 2) Closing other browser tabs, 3) Using a wired connection if possible. If the problem persists, let us know.
I can't hear Kelly's voice ▼
Make sure your device volume is up and not muted. Check that your browser has permission to play audio (look for a speaker icon in the address bar). Some browsers block autoplay audio – you may need to click to start.
