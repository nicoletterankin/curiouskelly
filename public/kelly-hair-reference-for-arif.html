📝 Detailed Description

Primary Base Color

Rich chestnut brown — a warm, deep brown with underlying red and golden undertones. Not black, not light brown. Think of freshly roasted chestnuts or rich dark chocolate with warmth. This is the dominant color making up approximately 70-80% of the hair.

Highlights

Subtle caramel and golden honey highlights woven throughout. These are not harsh streaks or bold balayage — they're natural-looking sun-kissed highlights that catch studio lighting beautifully. The highlights appear most prominently around the face, crown, and ends where light naturally hits.

Length & Volume

The hair flows past the shoulders with natural body and movement. Not thin or flat — it has healthy volume without being overly thick or poufy. The length allows for natural movement during animation while maintaining a professional, polished appearance.

Wave Pattern

Soft, natural waves — not tight curls, not straight. Think "slept on braids" or "beach waves" texture. The waves are loose and flowing, creating dimension and visual interest. They help catch light and create natural movement in video content.

Parting Style

Center-parted — this is locked in the visual identity. The center part gives Kelly a balanced, symmetrical look that works well for educational content where she faces the camera directly. The hair falls naturally on both sides of her face.

Texture & Finish

Natural, healthy shine — not matte, not overly glossy. The hair should look touchably soft with a subtle sheen that indicates health. Realistic hair texture with some natural variation, not plastic-looking or too perfect.

⚠️ Important Consistency Rules

🎯 Key Point

Kelly's hair color and style must remain consistent across ALL generated assets. The hair is one of the most recognizable features that establishes character identity.

  • DO: Match the chestnut brown base with warm caramel/honey highlights
  • DO: Maintain natural waves — soft and flowing, not tight curls
  • DO: Keep center parting for front-facing shots
  • DO: Ensure healthy shine and realistic texture
  • DON'T: Make hair black, blonde, or red-toned
  • DON'T: Use harsh streaks or unnatural highlight patterns
  • DON'T: Make hair perfectly straight or tightly curled
  • DON'T: Add hair accessories or dramatically change styling

👕 Sweater Specification (LOCKED)

⚠️ CRITICAL: Texture Matters

Kelly's sweater is **SOFT CASHMERE** with a fuzzy texture — NOT RIBBED. This is a locked specification. Any ribbed texture is incorrect.

✅ CORRECT: Soft Cashmere

  • Soft, fuzzy texture
  • Smooth surface appearance
  • Cozy, warm look
  • No visible knit pattern
  • Matte finish with subtle sheen

❌ INCORRECT: Ribbed

  • Visible vertical ridges
  • Defined knit texture
  • Cable knit patterns
  • Any repeating texture pattern
  • Waffle or thermal weave
