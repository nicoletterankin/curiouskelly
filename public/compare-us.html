📝 Our honest take

Kelly isn't trying to replace school, tutors, or other learning apps. We're building something specific: one thoughtful lesson per day, read aloud by an AI teacher, adapted for any age. That's it. If you want test prep, language fluency, or college courses—there are better tools for that. But if the goal is for the family to learn something interesting together every day, we might be a good fit.

Where we're not the right choice

  • Learning a specific skill deeply: If you want to become fluent in Spanish or master calculus, use Duolingo or Khan Academy. Kelly teaches breadth, not depth.
  • Test prep: We don't prep for SATs, AP exams, or certifications. Khan does this well.
  • Professional development: For career skills, LinkedIn Learning or Coursera are better fits.
  • No subscription needed: Khan Academy requires no payment and is excellent. If cost is a concern, start there.
  • Language learning: Duolingo is specifically built for this. We're not.
