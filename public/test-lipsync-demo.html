Step 1: We take Kelly's lesson scripts from the database.

Step 2: ElevenLabs generates audio using Kelly's trained voice.

Step 3: Forced alignment extracts phoneme timing (when each sound happens).

Step 4: Phonemes map to visemes (mouth shapes), which map to ARKit blendshapes.

Step 5: Pre-computed 30 FPS timeline stored in Supabase for instant playback.

Result: When Kelly speaks, her mouth moves naturally in sync with the audio - no runtime processing needed!
