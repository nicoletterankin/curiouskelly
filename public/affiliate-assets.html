💡 Best Practices

🎯
Be Authentic
Share your real experience. What lesson surprised you? What did your kids love? Authenticity converts better than polish.
📹
Show Don't Tell
Record yourself or your family using the app. Screen recordings of actual lessons perform incredibly well.
⏰
Post at Peak Times
Best times: 7-9am (morning routines), 12-1pm (lunch break), 7-9pm (family time). Weekends perform well for family content.
🔄
Evergreen Content
Create content that stays relevant. "Apps I use daily" videos continue earning months after posting.
📊
Track What Works
Notice which content types convert. Double down on what works for your audience.
⚖️
Disclose Partnership
Use #ad or #affiliate on paid posts. It's required by FTC and builds trust with your audience.
