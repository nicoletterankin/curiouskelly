Today marks the public launch of Curious Kelly, built by a co-founder of the Open Education movement to finish what open resources alone couldn't achieve—a daily teacher that meets learners where they are. Our Approach

One lesson per day. Short enough to finish. Designed for any learner.

Lesson of the Day PBC was founded by a co-founder of the Open Education movement to complete the mission—not just access to information, but access to a relationship. A daily companion who teaches, encourages, and grows with every learner.

The United Nations established SDG 4 in 2015: "Ensure inclusive and equitable quality education and promote lifelong learning opportunities for all."

The world heard "children." We heard "all."

Kelly teaches teenagers their first code and seniors their first video calls. Education isn't preparation for life—it IS life. And Kelly is here for all of it. Every day. Forever.
