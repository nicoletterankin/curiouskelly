Finishing What Open Education Started

The Open Education movement brought free educational resources to millions. But resources aren't enough. Completion rates for online courses hover around 5%. People don't finish. They don't form habits. They don't have a teacher who knows their name.

Lesson of the Day PBC was founded by a co-founder of that movement to complete the mission—not just access to information, but access to a relationship. A daily companion who teaches, encourages, and grows with every learner.

The United Nations established SDG 4 in 2015: "Ensure inclusive and equitable quality education and promote lifelong learning opportunities for all."

The world heard "children." We heard "all."

Kelly teaches 2-year-olds their first words and 92-year-olds their first video calls. Education isn't preparation for life—it IS life. And Kelly is here for all of it. Every day. Forever.
