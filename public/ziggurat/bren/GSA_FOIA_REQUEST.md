## REQUEST LETTER

**Date:** March 14, 2026

**To:** FOIA Officer, U.S. General Services Administration

**Re:** Freedom of Information Act Request - Chet Holifield Federal Building (GSA Property ID: CA0775ZZ)

Dear FOIA Officer:

Pursuant to the Freedom of Information Act, 5 U.S.C. § 552, I am requesting access to records pertaining to the Chet Holifield Federal Building, located at 24000 Avila Road, Laguna Niguel, California 92677 (GSA Property ID: CA0775ZZ).

### RECORDS REQUESTED

1. **Utilization Data (2015-2026)**
   - Building occupancy rates by year
   - Number of federal employees assigned to the building
   - Square footage occupied vs. vacant by agency
   - Utility consumption records (electricity, water, gas)

2. **Maintenance and Condition Reports**
   - Deferred maintenance backlog estimates
   - Building condition assessments
   - Capital improvement plans and estimates
   - Roof, HVAC, elevator, and structural system reports

3. **Disposal/Auction Records**
   - All documents related to the 2023, 2024, and 2025 auction processes
   - Bid submissions and evaluations
   - Reasons for auction cancellations or no-sale outcomes
   - Current asking price or reserve expectations

4. **Historic Preservation**
   - National Register of Historic Places eligibility determination (2015)
   - Section 106 consultation records
   - Historic preservation covenants or restrictions

5. **Environmental Records**
   - Phase I and Phase II environmental site assessments
   - Hazardous materials surveys (asbestos, lead, PCBs)
   - Remediation reports if applicable

6. **Operating Costs**
   - Annual operating budget for the facility (2020-2026)
   - Security costs
   - Maintenance contracts
