A Half-Century of History

1968
Construction begins for North American Rockwell Autonetics Division. William L. Pereira designs a stepped pyramid inspired by ancient Mesopotamian temples.
1971
Building completed but never occupied by Rockwell. Chairman deems it "too nice for an electronics firm."
1974
Rockwell trades the building to GSA for three surplus facilities. Federal government takes ownership.
2015
Determined eligible for the National Register of Historic Places. Pereira's original lobby interiors are preserved.
2025
IRS vacates. Building sits empty for the first time since 1974. Third auction expected.
20??
A new chapter. 237,800 SF of terrace canvas. Infinite possibilities.
