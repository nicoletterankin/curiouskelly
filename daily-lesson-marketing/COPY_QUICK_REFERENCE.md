## 💬 HOW WE TALK

### ✅ USE THESE WORDS
- Learn, discover, explore, wonder
- Daily, everyday, 8 minutes
- Age-adaptive, universal topic
- Safe, private, ad-free
- Simple, easy, effortless
- Curious, joyful, fascinating

### ❌ NEVER USE THESE
- ❌ **Free** (implies worthless—see `docs/brand/FORBIDDEN_WORD_FREE.md`)
- ❌ **Free trial** (use "7 days to explore")
- ❌ **Start free** (use "Start learning")
- ❌ Concierge
- ❌ Cohort
- ❌ Onboarding
- ❌ Founding members
- ❌ Pilot
- ❌ District-level
- ❌ Enrollment (use "sign up" or "start")

## ❓ FAQ ANSWERS (Copy-Paste Ready)

**Q: What happens after 7 days?**
A: Take 7 days to explore—no credit card required. Try unlimited lessons in all three languages. After that, it's just $4.99/month.

**Q: Can my whole family use one account?**
A: Yes! Create up to 5 profiles per subscription. Each person gets age-adaptive lessons.

**Q: What languages do you support?**
A: Every lesson is available in English, Spanish, and Brazilian Portuguese.

**Q: Is it really for ages 2 to 102?**
A: Yes! The same topic adapts to your age. A 6-year-old and 60-year-old can discuss the same lesson.

**Q: Can I gift this?**
A: Absolutely! $49.99 annual subscriptions make perfect gifts with email delivery.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
A: We never sell your data, show ads, or track you. Privacy-first means privacy always.

## 📊 WHAT CONVERTS BEST

Based on OpenEnglish model + best practices:

### High-Converting Headlines
- Lead with outcome: "Learn something new every day"
- Include time commitment: "8 minutes a day"
- Emphasize ease: "No credit card required"

### High-Converting CTAs
- Action verb + benefit: "Start Learning"
- Remove friction: "Take 7 Days to Explore"
- Gift angle: "Give 365 Days of Curiosity"

### Trust Signals
- "Trusted by learners in 47 countries"
- "Privacy-first, ad-free, no data selling"
- "Up to 5 family profiles included"
