✨ About Kelly

One lesson every day, for everyone

Kelly is an AI teacher built to solve UN Sustainable Development Goal 4: quality education for all. She teaches one topic daily, personalized to your age— from curious kids to lifelong learners.

What We Believe

The principles that guide everything we build.

🎯

Consistency Over Intensity

5 minutes daily beats 5 hours monthly. We design for habits, not heroics.

🔒

Privacy First

Your learning is personal. We never sell your data or use it for ads.

💡

Curiosity, Not Competition

Learning isn't a race. There are no leaderboards or social pressure.

🌱

Honest AI

Kelly is transparent about being AI. No manipulation, no dark patterns.

Built by Lesson of the Day PBC

We're a small team of educators, engineers, and designers who believe learning should be a daily habit, not a chore. We incorporated as a Public Benefit Corporation because we think education companies should be held to a higher standard.

Kelly started as a simple question: what if everyone in the world learned the same thing on the same day? What conversations would happen? What connections would form?
