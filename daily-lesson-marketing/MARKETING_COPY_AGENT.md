## Forbidden Language (Never Use)

❌ **"Concierge"** - Too luxury hotel, not education
❌ **"Cohort"** - Too academic/exclusive
❌ **"Onboarding"** - Too corporate
❌ **"Founding members"** - Creates false scarcity
❌ **"Pilot partners"** - Implies unfinished product
❌ **"District-level"** - No B2B school sales yet
❌ **"Enrollment"** - Too formal; use "sign up" or "start"
