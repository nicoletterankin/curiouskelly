### 1. Pricing Strategy
**DECISION:** Launch at $4.99/$49.99 (penetration pricing)
- Maximizes initial user acquisition
- Creates urgency with "founding member pricing"
- Plan to increase to $9.99/$99.99 at 90 days

### 2. Cash Flow Management
- Emphasize annual plans (front-loaded cash)
- Gift cards = immediate cash, deferred service
- Keep 3 months operating expenses in reserve
