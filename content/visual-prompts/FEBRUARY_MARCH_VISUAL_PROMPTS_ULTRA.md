Every image in this system serves a **cognitive scaffolding** purpose:

1. **Background (bg.png)** → Creates **situational context** that activates prior knowledge
2. **Hero (hero.png)** → Establishes **teacher presence** and models engagement
3. **Prop (prop.png)** → Provides **concrete anchor** for abstract concepts
4. **Guide-Point (guide-point.png)** → Directs **attentional focus** to key ideas
5. **Reaction (reaction.png)** → Models **emotional engagement** and wonder

### The Learning Sequence

```
bg → "Where are we?" (Context)
hero → "Who's teaching?" (Trust)
prop → "What does this look like?" (Concretization)
```

**Learning Objective:** Ice forms when water freezes and can shape entire landscapes.

### Visual Strategy
- Show the molecular reason: water molecules spacing out as they crystallize
- Show the consequence: ice floating, fish surviving below
- Celebrate the counterintuitive nature of this property

The cross-section shows WHY life survives winter: the ice floats, insulating the water below. Color palette: ice blues, aqua greens, warm underwater where life persists.
