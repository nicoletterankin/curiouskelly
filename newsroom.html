About Curious Kelly

Curious Kelly is an AI-powered educational platform that delivers personalized, age-adaptive lessons to learners ages 2-102. Through a combination of advanced voice synthesis (ElevenLabs), conversational AI (OpenAI), and 3D avatar technology (Unity/NVIDIA Audio2Face), Kelly serves as a personal teacher available 365 days a year.

The platform covers science, history, philosophy, art, and practical life skills through a carefully designed curriculum that adapts to each learner's pace and age. Curious Kelly is a Public Benefit Corporation committed to making education accessible, inclusive, and joyful for all.
