# Classroom Design Brief - Making Kelly OS Feel Like a Classroom

**Date:** December 2025  
**Purpose:** Visual direction for making Kelly OS feel warm, inviting, and educational  
**Audience:** v0 design team

---

## The Problem

**Current State:** UI feels "sanitary" and clinical, like a hospital or corporate app  
**Target State:** Warm, inviting classroom that evokes curiosity and wonder  
**Challenge:** Make it feel personal and safe for ages 2-102

---

## What Makes a Classroom?

### Visual Elements

**Warm Colors:**
- Not sterile white (#FFFFFF)
- Use warm grays (#F5F5F0, #E8E8E0)
- Accent with warm blues (#4A90E2), soft greens (#7FB069), warm oranges (#FF8C42)
- Avoid cold, clinical blues and grays

**Natural Textures:**
- Wood grain (subtle, not overwhelming)
- Paper texture (for backgrounds)
- Fabric textures (for soft elements)
- Chalkboard feel (for writing surfaces)

**Hand-Drawn Elements:**
- Chalkboard-style illustrations
- Hand-drawn sketches
- Imperfect, human lines
- Not perfectly geometric

**Personal Touches:**
- Kelly's personality shines through
- Personal notes and reflections
- Customizable spaces
- User's own content visible

**Curiosity-Inducing Visuals:**
- Wonder and discovery imagery
- Open books, maps, globes
- Science experiments
- Art supplies
- Natural elements (plants, light)

### Brand Personality

**Warm and Inviting:**
- Feels like a friend's cozy study
- Not intimidating or formal
- Welcoming to all ages

**Curious and Playful:**
- Encourages exploration
- Fun but not childish
- Serious learning, playful presentation

**Age-Appropriate (2-102):**
- Not too childish for adults
- Not too mature for kids
- Adapts to user's age

**Not Corporate/Sterile:**
- Avoids corporate design language
- No sterile white backgrounds
- No cold, geometric shapes
- No generic stock photos

**Educational but Fun:**
- Learning is the goal
- But it should feel enjoyable
- Not like homework or tests

### Emotions to Evoke

**Wonder and Curiosity:**
- "I want to explore this"
- "What's around the corner?"
- "This is fascinating"

**Comfort and Safety:**
- "I belong here"
- "It's safe to ask questions"
- "I won't be judged"

**Excitement to Learn:**
- "I can't wait to learn more"
- "This is interesting"
- "I want to dive deeper"

**Belonging and Community:**
- "I'm part of something"
- "Others are learning too"
- "We're in this together"

**Achievement and Progress:**
- "I'm making progress"
- "I can do this"
- "I'm growing"

## Lens Renaming Strategy

### Current → Suggested

| Current | Suggested | Rationale |
|---------|-----------|-----------|
| **Calendar** | **Adventure Map** | More playful, journey-focused, invites exploration |
| **Library** | **Discovery Shelf** | Feels like browsing a real bookshelf, invites curiosity |
| **Atlas** | **Wonder Gallery** | More magical, less clinical, evokes wonder |
| **Journal** | **Memory Book** | Personal, warm, feels like a keepsake |
| **Chat** | **Ask Kelly** | Already good, keep as-is (personal, direct) |
| **Quiz** | **Check Your Thinking** | Less test-like, more reflective, less intimidating |
| **Achievements** | **Milestones** | Less gamified, more meaningful, celebrates growth |
| **Settings** | **Your Space** | Personal, inviting, feels like customization not configuration |

### Design Principles

**Use Warm, Inviting Language:**
- "Adventure Map" vs "Calendar"
- "Discovery Shelf" vs "Library"
- "Your Space" vs "Settings"

**Avoid Corporate/Sterile Terms:**
- ❌ "Dashboard"
- ❌ "Settings"
- ❌ "Analytics"
- ❌ "Configuration"

**Make It Feel Personal and Safe:**
- "Your Space" (not "Settings")
- "Memory Book" (not "Journal")
- "My Profile" (not "Account")

**Encourage Curiosity and Exploration:**
- "Adventure Map" (invites exploration)
- "Discovery Shelf" (invites browsing)
- "Wonder Gallery" (invites wonder)

**Age-Appropriate for 2-102:**
- Not too childish ("Adventure Map" works for all ages)
- Not too mature ("Your Space" is friendly)
- Adapts to user's age (same names, different presentation)

## Visual Design Direction

### Color Palette

**Primary:**
- Warm Gray: `#F5F5F0` (background)
- Warm Blue: `#4A90E2` (primary accent)
- Soft Green: `#7FB069` (success, growth)
- Warm Orange: `#FF8C42` (energy, curiosity)

**Secondary:**
- Paper White: `#FAFAF5` (cards, surfaces)
- Chalkboard: `#2C3E50` (text, contrast)
- Wood: `#D4A574` (accents, warmth)
- Sky Blue: `#87CEEB` (light, airy)

**Avoid:**
- ❌ Pure white (#FFFFFF)
- ❌ Cold grays (#808080)
- ❌ Corporate blues (#0066CC)
- ❌ Neon colors

### Typography

**Headings:**
- Warm, friendly sans-serif
- Slightly rounded (not geometric)
- Examples: Inter, Poppins, Nunito

**Body:**
- Readable, comfortable
- Not too formal
- Examples: Inter, Open Sans, Lato

**Handwriting Elements:**
- Use sparingly for personal touches
- Chalkboard-style for notes
- Examples: Kalam, Caveat, Permanent Marker

### Imagery

**Use:**
- ✅ Natural light (warm, golden)
- ✅ Real objects (books, plants, art supplies)
- ✅ People learning (diverse, happy)
- ✅ Hand-drawn illustrations
- ✅ Cozy spaces (studies, libraries, classrooms)

**Avoid:**
- ❌ Stock photos (generic, sterile)
- ❌ Perfect geometric shapes
- ❌ Cold, clinical imagery
- ❌ Corporate photography

### Layout

**Spacing:**
- Generous whitespace (but warm, not sterile)
- Comfortable reading distances
- Not cramped, not too sparse

**Grid:**
- Organic, not rigid
- Slight imperfections welcome
- Not perfectly aligned (human touch)

**Cards/Surfaces:**
- Soft shadows (warm, not harsh)
- Slight texture (paper, fabric)
- Rounded corners (friendly, not sharp)

## Component-Specific Guidance

### Adventure Map (Calendar)

**Visual:**
- Map-like layout
- Journey paths between days
- Milestone markers
- Warm, earthy colors

**Interaction:**
- Hover reveals lesson preview
- Click to start adventure
- Progress trails show path taken

**Feel:**
- Like exploring a map
- Like planning a journey
- Like marking milestones

### Discovery Shelf (Library)

**Visual:**
- Bookshelf layout
- Book spines visible
- Categories as shelves
- Warm wood tones

**Interaction:**
- Browse like a real shelf
- Pull out books to explore
- Organize your collection

**Feel:**
- Like browsing a library
- Like discovering new books
- Like building a collection

### Wonder Gallery (Visual Atlas)

**Visual:**
- Gallery wall layout
- Art-style frames
- Light, airy space
- Warm gallery lighting

**Interaction:**
- Browse like an art gallery
- Click to explore details
- Collections and themes

**Feel:**
- Like visiting a museum
- Like appreciating art
- Like discovering beauty

### Memory Book (Journal)

**Visual:**
- Book-like layout
- Handwritten-style text
- Personal touches
- Warm, paper texture

**Interaction:**
- Write like a journal
- Add photos and memories
- Reflect on learning

**Feel:**
- Like keeping a diary
- Like preserving memories
- Like personal reflection

### Your Space (Settings)

- Personalizable spaces

**Interaction:**

**Feel:**
