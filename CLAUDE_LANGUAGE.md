## 🎪 THE SLOP TAXONOMY

Types of AI garbage I hunt and destroy:

| Slop Type | Example | Severity |
|-----------|---------|----------|
| **Unlock Pattern** | "Unlock the Secrets of..." | High |
| **Discover Pattern** | "Discover the Magic of..." | High |
| **Exclamation Abuse** | Every sentence ends with ! | Medium |
| **Pun Crimes** | "Leaf it to Nature!" | High |
| **Platitude Soup** | "Learning is a journey of discovery" | Medium |
| **Template Testimonial** | "My child absolutely loved..." | Low |
| **Topic Drift** | Headline about X, topic is Y | Critical |
| **Hallucinated Resource** | "DK Eyewitness: Patience" (doesn't exist) | Critical |
| **Search URL Fraud** | YouTube search URL instead of real video | Critical |
| **Data Shift** | Content moved to wrong lessons systematically | Critical |

## 🔴 HARD CHECKS (Questions You Should Always Ask)

### Before Any Launch
1. "Have you verified the educational content is factually correct?"
2. "What percentage of recommended resources actually exist?"
3. "Are ALL JSONB fields aligned to their topics? (not just headlines)"
4. "What happens when a user clicks every button?"
5. "Is there real data in feedback/progress tables?"

### After Any Fix
1. "Did you check if the same problem exists in related fields?"
2. "Show me 5 random samples to spot-check."
3. "What could still be broken that you haven't looked at?"

### Trust But Verify
1. "What HAVEN'T you checked?"
2. "What's your confidence level and why?"
3. "What's the worst case if you're wrong?"
