Checklist before marking a clip approved:
1. Lip-sync matches audio (no drift past ±30 ms).
2. Eyes stay locked on camera unless a scripted look-away is intentional.
3. Hands/props remain inside frame; no shoulder or chair clipping.
4. Lighting matches the reference scene; no color temperature jumps.
5. Render settings: 1080p60, correct codec, no dropped frames.
