## 🎨 CONTENT PILLARS

### 1. 🤯 "WAIT, WHAT?" — Mind-Blowing Facts
*The shareable hook*

**Format:** 15-second vertical video, carousel, tweet thread

**Examples:**
- "Your brain can't tell the difference between doing something and vividly imagining it. That's not philosophy — it's neuroscience."
- "Octopuses have 9 brains. One central brain, and 8 mini-brains in each arm."
- "The inventor of the Pringles can is buried in one."

**Visual Style:** 
- Bold text on dark background
- Kelly's face showing genuine surprise
- Quick cuts, punchy transitions
- Sound: Record scratch → revelation sound

---

### 2. 🎭 "WHAT WOULD YOU DO?" — Interactive Scenarios
*The engagement magnet*

**Format:** Instagram Story polls, TikTok duets, Twitter polls

**Examples:**
- "A trolley is heading toward 5 people. You can pull a lever to divert it to kill 1 person. What do you do?"
- "You can know exactly when you'll die, or never know. Which do you choose?"
- "Would you rather lose all your memories or never be able to make new ones?"

**Visual Style:**
- Split screen with options
- Kelly contemplating
- Animated choice indicators
- Sound: Tense, thoughtful music

---

### 3. 🧪 "TRY THIS NOW" — Instant Experiments
*The participation driver*

**Format:** 30-60 second how-to, Reels, Shorts

**Examples:**
- "Close your eyes. Picture a lemon. Now imagine biting into it. Did your mouth water? That's visualization."
- "Hold your breath and see how long you can last. Now try after taking 3 deep breaths. Notice the difference?"
- "Look at your phone for 30 seconds. Now close your eyes and try to draw it from memory."

**Visual Style:**
- Kelly demonstrating
- Timer on screen
- Results reveal
- Sound: Upbeat, experimental

---

### 4. 🌍 "ACROSS TIME & SPACE" — Historical & Cultural Deep Dives
*The wonder generator*

**Format:** Long-form carousel, YouTube explainer, email deep-dive

**Examples:**
- "In 1977, we sent a golden record into space. Here's what's on it..."
- "There's a library in Turkey that's 2,600 years old. It's still standing."
- "The world's oldest tree is 5,000 years old. It was already 1,000 years old when the pyramids were built."

**Visual Style:**
- Epic imagery, archival footage
- Timeline animations
- Kelly as narrator/guide
- Sound: Orchestral, wonder-inspiring

---

### 5. 💡 "THE SKILL" — Daily GROW Track Content
*The practical value*

**Format:** Infographic, carousel, short video

**Examples:**
- "The 2-Minute Rule: If it takes less than 2 minutes, do it now."
- "The Feynman Technique: Learn anything by teaching it to a 5-year-old."
- "The 5-Second Rule: Count down from 5, then act before your brain talks you out of it."

**Visual Style:**
- Clean, minimal design
- Step-by-step visuals
- Kelly demonstrating
- Sound: Focused, productive
