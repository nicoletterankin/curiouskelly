## Quick Start: 5-Minute Daily Prep

### Before Class (2 minutes)

1. **Open today's Teacher Guide** (one-page PDF)
2. **Read the 3 discussion prompts**
3. **Note the extension activity** (optional)

### During Class (8-10 minutes)

1. **Play the lesson** (Kelly teaches)
2. **Pause at Socratic moments** (add your own questions)
3. **Facilitate brief discussion** (student responses)

### After Class (1 minute)

1. **Optional:** Assign extension activity
2. **Optional:** Note observations for parent pulse

## Discussion Facilitation Tips

### The Socratic Moment

When Kelly asks the Socratic question:

**DO:**
- Pause the lesson
- Ask students to think quietly for 10 seconds
- Take 3-4 responses before resuming
- Validate ALL answers ("Interesting thinking!")

**DON'T:**
- Rush to the "right" answer
- Let one student dominate
- Give your own answer first

### Example Script

```
Kelly: "If the Moon doesn't make its own light, why can we see it?"

You: "Interesting question! Let's think about this. 
      What do you think? [Pause]
      I want to hear from at least 3 people."

Student 1: "Something shines on it?"
You: "Good thinking! You're on to something."

Student 2: "The Sun reflects off it?"
You: "Reflection—great word! Anyone else?"

Student 3: "I don't know..."
You: "That's okay! Wondering is how we learn. 
      Let's see what Kelly says..."

[Resume lesson]
```

### The Wonder Moment

When Kelly asks the critical thinking question:

**Goal:** Students practice questioning, not just answering

```
Kelly: "How do scientists KNOW the Sun is 93 million miles away?"

You: "Before we hear Kelly's answer, what do YOU think?
      How would you even begin to measure that?"

[Take responses—focus on the THINKING process, not the answer]

You: "Great thinking! Notice you all started asking questions
      and imagining ways to figure it out. THAT'S the skill 
      we're building here."
```

## Assessment Made Easy

### Built-In Assessment

Every lesson includes:
- **Socratic response quality** (observed)
- **Choice selection** (logged automatically)
- **Recall prompts** (3 and 7 days later)

### Monthly Rubric Template

| Skill | Developing (1) | Approaching (2) | Proficient (3) | Advanced (4) |
|-------|----------------|-----------------|----------------|--------------|
| **Engagement** | Rarely participates | Sometimes participates | Usually participates | Leads discussions |
| **Critical Thinking** | Accepts claims without question | Questions occasionally | Regularly asks "how do we know?" | Independently verifies |
| **Application** | Cannot connect to life | Sometimes connects | Usually connects | Creates novel connections |

### Portfolio Prompts (Monthly)

End of each month, students answer:
1. What's one thing that surprised you this month?
2. What's one question you still have?
3. How did you use something you learned outside of class?
