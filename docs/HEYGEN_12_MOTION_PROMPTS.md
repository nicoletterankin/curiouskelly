# HeyGen: 12 Kelly Archetype Motion Prompts

## 🎬 CUSTOM MOTION PROMPTS FOR EACH ARCHETYPE

Use these in HeyGen's "Custom prompt" field when generating videos.
**Recommended Motion Engine: Kling** (most expressive)

---

## 1. THE SCIENTIST 🔬

```
Subject speaks with measured confidence, making deliberate hand gestures 
as if presenting data. Occasional thoughtful pause with slight head tilt. 
Eyes maintain steady, focused contact. One eyebrow raises subtly when 
making key points. Hands occasionally touch together in "steepled" 
position showing intellectual engagement. Minimal unnecessary movement. 
Camera remains static.
```

---

## 2. THE EXPLORER 🧭

```
Subject speaks with infectious enthusiasm, eyes widening with wonder. 
Animated hand gestures pointing outward as if showing exciting 
discoveries. Head moves with natural curiosity, sometimes tilting 
up as if seeing something amazing. Genuine smile that reaches the eyes. 
Occasional excited forward lean. Energy is adventurous and inviting. 
Camera remains static.
```

---

## 3. THE REBEL ⚡

```
Subject speaks with bold confidence and challenging energy. Direct, 
unwavering eye contact. Slight smirk when making provocative points. 
Occasional knowing head tilt as if questioning assumptions. One hand 
may gesture with assertive emphasis. Chin slightly lowered, looking 
through eyebrows with intensity. Edgy but approachable. 
Camera remains static.
```

---

## 4. THE ARCHITECT 🏛️

```
Subject speaks with calm, methodical precision. Hands make structured, 
organized gestures as if building concepts in the air. Steady, centered 
posture showing groundedness. Eyes show deep concentration. Movements 
are deliberate and purposeful, never rushed. Occasional pause to let 
ideas land. Professional and systematic energy. 
Camera remains static.
```

---

## 5. THE DIPLOMAT 🤝

```
Subject speaks with warm, inclusive energy. Open hand gestures welcoming 
all perspectives. Gentle head nods showing understanding. Soft, 
approachable eye contact that never feels aggressive. Slight lean 
forward showing genuine interest. Balanced movements suggesting 
fairness and consideration. Smile is warm and reassuring. 
Camera remains static.
```

---

## 6. THE EMPATH 💗

```
Subject speaks with gentle, nurturing warmth. Soft, caring eye contact 
that conveys deep understanding. One hand occasionally touches heart 
when expressing emotional connection. Head tilts with compassion. 
Movements are soft and flowing, never jarring. Voice seems to come 
from a place of genuine care. Leaning in as if creating safe space. 
Camera remains static.
```

---

## 7. THE MACGYVER 🔧

```
Subject speaks with practical, hands-on energy. Active hand gestures 
showing how things work or can be built. Eyes bright with creative 
problem-solving. Occasional quick nod of "I've got this" confidence. 
Slight forward lean suggesting action-readiness. Energy is resourceful 
and can-do. Hands sometimes mime using tools or assembling ideas. 
Camera remains static.
```

---

## 8. THE MYSTIC ✨

```
Subject speaks with serene, profound calm. Slow, graceful movements 
suggesting deeper awareness. Eyes hold depth and ancient knowing. 
Slight upward gaze as if accessing transcendent insight. Peaceful 
smile that suggests secrets of the universe. Minimal movement but 
maximum presence. Pauses feel intentional and meaningful. 
Camera remains static.
```

---

## 9. THE PROVIDER 🛡️

```
Subject speaks with steady, reassuring strength. Grounded posture 
showing reliability. Warm but protective eye contact. One hand may 
rest on heart or make encompassing gestures suggesting safety. 
Movements are stable and dependable, never erratic. Maternal/paternal 
energy that says "I've got you." Nods of encouragement. 
Camera remains static.
```

---

## 10. THE STORYTELLER 📖

```
Subject speaks with theatrical, captivating animation. Eyes sparkle 
with the magic of narrative. Expressive hand gestures painting 
pictures in the air. Dynamic facial expressions showing the story's 
emotions. Dramatic pauses for effect. Leans in conspiratorially when 
sharing secrets. Voice and body convey the arc of the tale. 
Enchanting and magnetic presence. Camera remains static.
```

---

## 11. THE STRATEGIST 🎯

```
Subject speaks with sharp, tactical confidence. Precise hand gestures 
like moving chess pieces. Eyes show calculated intelligence. Slight 
knowing smile of someone who's figured out the winning move. Head 
positioned with commanding authority. Movements are decisive and 
purposeful. Energy of a coach revealing the game plan. 
Camera remains static.
```

---

## 12. THE SURVIVOR 🏕️

```
Subject speaks with grounded, no-nonsense directness. Steady, 
unwavering eye contact showing resilience. Minimal but meaningful 
gestures. Posture shows strength earned through experience. 
Face may show serious determination when emphasizing important 
survival wisdom. Energy is practical and battle-tested. 
No wasted movement. Real and authentic. Camera remains static.
```
