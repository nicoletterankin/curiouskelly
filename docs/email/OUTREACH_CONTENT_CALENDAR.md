# 📅 Email Outreach & Content Calendar

## The Big Picture

**Email is how Kelly stays in learners' lives between lessons.**

Every email should:
1. ✨ Feel like Kelly wrote it personally
2. 📚 Teach something (even if small)
3. 💫 Delight or surprise
4. 🎯 Have ONE clear purpose

---

## Weekly Email Schedule

| Day | Email Type | Audience | Purpose |
|-----|------------|----------|---------|

## A/B Testing Ideas

### Subject Lines
Test variations like:
- Question vs Statement: "Did you know?" vs "Here's something wild"
- Emoji vs No Emoji: "🎉 You did it!" vs "You did it!"
- Personalization: "[Name], you're on fire" vs "You're on fire"

### Send Times
Test:
- Morning (7am) vs Evening (7pm)
- Weekday vs Weekend
- Consistent time vs Varied times

### Content
Test:
- Long vs Short emails
- Single CTA vs Multiple CTAs
- Fun fact in email vs in signature
