## The Five Pillars at a Glance

### 1️⃣ Socratic Mode
> Kelly asks before she teaches

**Current:** "The Moon reflects light."
**Future:** "If the Moon doesn't make light, why can we see it? [choices]"

### 2️⃣ Critical Thinking (Wonder & Verify)
> Challenge learners to question how we know

**Added prompt type:** "How do scientists KNOW this? What evidence convinced them?"

### 3️⃣ AI Fluency Track
> 12 monthly lessons on learning with AI

**Lesson 1:** "I'm an AI" — Kelly explains her nature, limitations, and how to use AI wisely

### 4️⃣ Parent Companion (Parent Pulse)
> Daily email connecting learning to family

**Content:** Summary + Conversation Starter + Extension Activity + Book Recommendation

### 5️⃣ AI Transparency
> Every lesson includes an "I'm an AI" moment

**Categories:** Limitation | Error Possibility | Experience Gap | Self-Awareness
