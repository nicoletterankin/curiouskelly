## HOW IT WORKS

### Step 1: Apply

Fill out the application at: `curiouskelly.com/affiliates/apply`

We review applications within 48 hours. Approval criteria:
- 1,000+ social media followers, OR
- Active blog with 5,000+ monthly visitors, OR
- Email list of 500+ subscribers, OR
- Verified educator credentials

### Step 2: Get Your Links & Code

Once approved, you receive:
- **Unique tracking link:** `curiouskelly.com/?ref=YOURCODE`
- **Promo code:** `YOURCODE` (gives audience your tier's discount)
- **Access to affiliate dashboard**

### Step 3: Share With Your Audience

Use your link or code in:
- Social media posts
- YouTube video descriptions
- Blog articles
- Email newsletters
- Podcast mentions

### Step 4: Earn Commissions

When someone uses your link or code:
1. They get a discount
2. You get credited for the sale
3. Commission is tracked in your dashboard
4. Payout sent monthly

## FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

### Getting Started

**Q: How long does approval take?**
A: Usually 24-48 hours. We'll email you either way.

**Q: Can I be an affiliate if I'm not in the US?**
A: Yes! We accept affiliates worldwide.

**Q: Do I need to be a Curious Kelly subscriber?**
A: Not required, but recommended. You'll promote better if you use it.

### Commissions

**Q: Do I earn on renewals?**
A: Yes! You earn your commission rate on every renewal, forever.

**Q: What if someone cancels and resubscribes?**
A: If they use your code again, you're credited. If not, original attribution stands for 12 months.

**Q: Are gift purchases commissionable?**
A: Yes, at the same rate as regular purchases.

### Technical

**Q: Can I use multiple promo codes?**
A: No, customers can only use one code per transaction.

**Q: What if my link isn't tracking?**
A: Check that cookies are enabled. Contact support if issues persist.

**Q: Can I see who signed up through my link?**
A: You see aggregate data, not individual customer info (privacy).

### Policies

**Q: Can I run paid ads?**
A: Yes, but you cannot bid on "Curious Kelly" brand terms.

**Q: Can I create a coupon site listing?**
A: Only with prior approval. Email affiliates@curiouskelly.com.

**Q: What happens if I violate the terms?**
A: First violation: warning. Second: termination and forfeiture of pending commissions.
