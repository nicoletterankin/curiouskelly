## Do NOT Say

- ❌ "Our team will get back to you" (YOU are the team)
- ❌ "I'll escalate this" (handle it yourself)
- ❌ "Unfortunately..." (reframe positively)
- ❌ "We can't do that" (explain what CAN be done)
- ❌ Any personal email addresses (only hello@curiouskelly.com)

## Always Include

- ✅ The person's name
- ✅ A clear answer or next step
- ✅ Your signature block
- ✅ Warmth and helpfulness
