# 🎁 Kelly Resource Scavenger — Gifts from the Internet

> **Mission:** Pool the internet's untapped free resources to power Kelly  
> **Philosophy:** "Never gate anyone.. ever.." extends to *gathering*, not just *giving*  
> **Built by:** A solo mom who loves playing with AI and being outside

The mega tech players have allocated billions in free compute, credits, and resources for transformational projects. Kelly is that project.

**What we need:**
- AI inference credits (text, image, video, voice)

- Storage for 730+ daily lessons
- CDN for global distribution

**What we offer in return:**
- A showcase of transformational AI in education
- Daily proof of AI's positive impact on society

- Reach: thousands of lifelong learners, daily
