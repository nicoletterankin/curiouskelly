# FORBIDDEN WORD: "Free"

## Why We Never Say "Free"

**This is a non-negotiable brand principle. No exceptions.**

---

## The Problem with "Free"

### 1. "Free" Implies Worthless
Our brains are wired: **price = value**. When you say something is "free," you're subconsciously telling people it's worth nothing. But education is *priceless*, not worthless.

### 2. "Free" Creates a Transactional Mindset
It positions the learner as a consumer getting a deal, rather than a human being receiving what they deserve. It cheapens the sacred relationship between teacher and learner.

### 3. "Free" is a Tech-Bro Manipulation Word
In 2025, everyone knows "free" means "you are the product." Data harvesting, attention extraction, upselling. The word is **tainted** by a decade of surveillance capitalism.

### 4. "Free" Sounds Like Charity
It's condescending—"free lunch for the poor kids." Education shouldn't be a handout. It's a **birthright**.

### 5. "Free" Erases the Value of Creators
Nothing is truly free. Teachers worked. Creators poured their souls in. Saying "free" makes their contribution invisible.

### 6. Libraries Don't Shout "FREE BOOKS"
They just... exist. They're part of the commons. That's the energy we want.

---

## Philosophy: Education as Commons

Education is not a transaction. It's not a coupon. It's not a deal.

**Education is a birthright. Kelly is here for you. Period.**

We don't brag about what it costs (or doesn't cost). We describe what you GET, not what you DON'T pay.

## Replacement Patterns

### Instead of "Free tier" or "Free plan"
| ❌ Don't Say | ✅ Say Instead |
|-------------|----------------|
| Free | Today |
| Free tier | Daily |
| Free plan | Open |
| Free access | Yours |

**Example:** 
- ❌ "Free - $0"
- ✅ "Today - $0" or just "Today's lesson"

### Instead of "Start Free" / "Get Started Free"
| ❌ Don't Say | ✅ Say Instead |
|-------------|----------------|
| Start Free | Start Learning |
| Get Started Free | Begin |
| Try it free | Try a lesson |
| Start learning free | Start today |

### Instead of "Free trial"
| ❌ Don't Say | ✅ Say Instead |
|-------------|----------------|
| 7-day free trial | 7 days to explore |
| Free trial | Your first week |
| Start your free trial | Start your journey |
| Is it free? | How do I start? |

### Instead of "Today's lesson, always free"
| ❌ Don't Say | ✅ Say Instead |
|-------------|----------------|
| Today's lesson, always free | Today's lesson is yours |
| Free forever | Always here for you |
| One free lesson every day | One lesson. Every day. Yours. |

### Instead of "No cost" / "At no charge"
| ❌ Don't Say | ✅ Say Instead |
|-------------|----------------|
| No cost | Included |
| At no charge | Part of your journey |
| Free of charge | Available to everyone |

## Approved CTAs (No "Free")

### Primary CTAs
- **"Start Learning"**
- **"Begin"**
- **"Start Today"**
- **"Try a Lesson"**
- **"Start Your Journey"**

### Trial-related CTAs
- **"Take 7 days to explore"**
- **"Your first week is on us"** (acceptable alternative)
- **"Explore for 7 days"**

### Pricing Section Language
- **"Today"** instead of "Free" tier name
- **"Today's lesson is yours"** instead of "Today's lesson, always free"
- **"$0"** is acceptable (it's a number, not a loaded word)

## Edge Cases

### When Describing Competitors
When writing comparison copy (e.g., "Khan Academy is free"), you may state facts about competitors' pricing model, but frame it neutrally:

| ❌ Don't Say | ✅ Say Instead |
|-------------|----------------|
| Khan Academy is free | Khan Academy: $0/year |
| Khan Academy is completely free | Khan Academy requires no subscription |

### When Users Ask "Is it free?"
Answer the question honestly without using the word:

| ❌ Don't Say | ✅ Say Instead |
|-------------|----------------|
| Yes, it's free! | Today's lesson is always yours, no payment needed. |
| The free trial is really free | You can explore for 7 days without paying anything. |

### Legal/Terms Contexts
In legal contexts (Terms of Service, license grants), the word "royalty-free" has a specific legal meaning and may be used where legally necessary. However, marketing copy should never use it.

## Enforcement

### Pre-Publish Checklist
Before publishing ANY marketing copy, check:

- [ ] No use of "free" in headlines
- [ ] No use of "free" in CTAs
- [ ] No use of "free" in meta descriptions
- [ ] No use of "free" in pricing tier names
- [ ] No use of "free trial" anywhere

### Validation Tool
Add to copy validation:
```javascript
const FORBIDDEN_FREE_PATTERNS = [
  /\bfree\b/i,
  /\bstart\s+free\b/i,
  /\bget\s+started\s+free\b/i,
  /\btry\s+it\s+free\b/i,
  /\bfree\s+trial\b/i,
  /\bfor\s+free\b/i,
];
```

### Exception Process
If you believe "free" must be used (e.g., quoting a legal term), document the reason and get explicit approval before publishing.

## Why This Matters

Curious Kelly is not a startup chasing growth metrics. We are building something timeless.

The words we choose shape how learners feel about their own education. When we say "free," we accidentally tell them their learning has no value.

**Education is priceless. Not worthless. Not free.**

Today's lesson is **yours**.
