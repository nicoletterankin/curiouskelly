# TRUST & SAFETY TEAM CHARTER

> *"Our job is to ask the hard questions before our users have to."*

---

## Mission

To ensure Curious Kelly is a psychologically safe learning environment by proactively identifying risks, maintaining transparency about AI-generated content, and protecting user wellbeing above engagement.

---

## Scope

The Trust & Safety team has authority and responsibility over:

1. **Simulated Social Content** — All AI-generated peer interactions
2. **Disclosure Systems** — How we mark and explain simulated content
3. **User Controls** — Settings and preferences for social features
4. **Harm Monitoring** — Watching for negative psychological impacts
5. **Policy Development** — Guidelines for ethical feature development
6. **Incident Response** — Handling trust violations
7. **External Relations** — Audits, research partnerships, regulatory compliance

## Responsibilities

### 1. Pre-Launch Review

**Mandatory review for:**
- Any feature involving simulated social content
- Any feature showing user-generated content
- Any feature that could affect psychological wellbeing
- Any feature targeting vulnerable populations (children, seniors)
- Any change to disclosure or control systems

**Review checklist:**
- [ ] Harm Assessment Framework completed
- [ ] Disclosure requirements met
- [ ] User controls implemented
- [ ] Documentation updated
- [ ] Monitoring plan in place

**Authority:**
- T&S can block launches that fail review
- Escalation to CEO if disagreement with product

### 2. Ongoing Monitoring

**What we monitor:**

| Signal | Source | Threshold |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| "Didn't know it was simulated" | Support tickets, surveys | Any report |
| Compulsive usage patterns | Analytics | TBD based on research |
| Negative sentiment about simulation | App reviews, social media | Pattern of complaints |
| Psychological harm reports | Support tickets | Any report |
| Feature misuse | Analytics | TBD |

**Frequency:**
- Daily: Scan support tickets
- Weekly: Review analytics dashboards
- Monthly: Deep dive on trends
- Quarterly: External audit

### 3. Incident Response

**Severity Levels:**

| Level | Definition | Response Time | Who's Involved |
|-------|------------|---------------|----------------|
| **Critical** | User harm reported, widespread deception | 1 hour | CEO, T&S, Engineering |
| **High** | Pattern of user confusion, potential harm | 24 hours | T&S, Product, Engineering |
| **Medium** | Individual complaints, unclear impact | 1 week | T&S, Product |
| **Low** | Minor policy questions, edge cases | 2 weeks | T&S |

**Response Protocol:**

1. **Identify**: What happened? Who's affected?
2. **Contain**: Stop the harm from spreading
3. **Communicate**: Inform affected users if needed
4. **Fix**: Deploy solution
5. **Review**: What went wrong? How do we prevent?
6. **Document**: Update policies and processes

### 4. Policy Development

**Domains:**
- Simulated content guidelines
- Disclosure standards
- User control requirements
- Age-appropriate design
- Vulnerable user protections
- Data use for social features

**Process:**
1. Identify need (research, incident, new feature)
2. Draft policy
3. Review with stakeholders
4. Publish internally
5. Train teams
6. Monitor compliance

### 5. External Relations

**Audits:**
- Annual third-party ethics audit
- Quarterly disclosure compliance check
- Ad-hoc audits for new features

**Research Partnerships:**
- Academic researchers studying AI in education
- Child development experts
- Digital wellbeing researchers

**Regulatory:**
- Monitor AI regulation developments
- Prepare for disclosure requirements
- Engage with policymakers constructively

## Governance

### Decision Rights

| Decision | T&S Authority |
|----------|---------------|
| Block feature launch | Yes, with escalation path |
| Require disclosure changes | Yes |
| Mandate user controls | Yes |
| Remove live feature | Yes, with CEO notification |
| Policy changes | Yes, with stakeholder review |
| Hire for T&S team | Yes, with budget approval |

### Reporting

**To CEO:** Monthly summary + immediate for Critical incidents
**To Board:** Quarterly trust & safety report
**Public:** Annual transparency report (2027+)

### Independence

- T&S reports to CEO, not Product
- T&S has veto on safety-critical launches
- T&S budget is protected from cuts
- T&S metrics are separate from engagement metrics

## Metrics

### What We Track

**Health Metrics (Good):**
- % users who understand content is simulated (survey)
- User satisfaction with disclosure (survey)
- % users who've customized social settings
- Learning outcomes (primary product metric)

**Warning Metrics (Bad):**
- Reports of feeling deceived
- Compulsive usage patterns
- Psychological harm reports
- Disclosure comprehension failures

### What We DON'T Track for T&S

- Engagement with simulated content
- Time on app
- "Retention" of social features

*These would create perverse incentives.*

### Required Training (All Employees)

1. **Trust & Safety Overview** (1 hour)
   - Why T&S exists
   - What simulated content is
   - How to report concerns

   - Harm Assessment Framework
   - Disclosure requirements
   - Dark pattern recognition
   - Vulnerable user considerations

   - What we can/can't simulate
   - How to mark content
   - Technical implementation

- Monthly T&S office hours
- Quarterly case study reviews
- Annual external speaker on digital ethics
