# UI AUDIT - December 20, 2025
## Kelly's Classroom Cleanup

---

## THE VISION

**Two Curriculum Tracks:**
- **LEARN**: 365 traditional topic lessons
- **GROW**: 365 AI fluency lessons  
- **TOTAL**: 730 lessons, each with 7 phases

**Header After Cleanup:**
- Logo (Kelly) → Opens Kelly Panel
- Time/Date → Opens Calendar
- THAT'S IT. Everything else moves.

**Kelly Panel = The Heart of the Classroom:**
- Lesson title + marketing line
- Learn/Grow toggle (moved from header)
- Phase navigation (expanded, detailed, hoverable teaching moments)
- Lesson visuals
- Simulated student comments (hundreds per phase)
- Reactions (Got it/Wow/More)
- Chat with Kelly

**Pause Behavior:**
- When paused → Kelly panel + calendar panels "open and freeze"
- Like a video game pause menu

## HEADER ZONE

### 1. Kelly Logo/Avatar (Top Left)
**Current:** Opens side panel with "Live Chat"
**Problem:** Says "Live Chat" - feels disconnected from lesson content

**NEW VISION: THE KELLY PANEL**
When you click Kelly's avatar, you're clicking on HER PROFILE. She's determined to talk about TODAY'S LESSON.

The Kelly Panel should contain:
1. **Header**: Today's lesson title (e.g., "Why You Get Up") + marketing tagline
2. **Phase Navigation**: The 7 phase dots (moved FROM header)
3. **Lesson Visuals**: High-quality AI visuals for each phase (moved FROM side rail)
4. **Student Comments**: Hundreds of simulated comments per phase
   - Designed to TEACH
   - Designed to make you LAUGH
   - Designed to make the topic FUN
   - Like social media comments that keep you engaged for 20 minutes
5. **Reactions**: "Got it" / "Wow" / "More" pills (moved FROM bottom of screen)
6. **Chat Input**: Way to actually talk to Kelly

**Interaction Design:**
- Panel should pop DOWN (not slide over)
- Roll down / roll up animation
- Feels like clicking on a creator's profile on social media

**Content Creation Task:**
- [ ] Write hundreds of comments per phase
- [ ] Comments are SIMULATED but designed to teach
- [ ] Must follow Trust & Safety guidelines (✨ disclosure)
