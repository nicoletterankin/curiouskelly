## CRITICAL NOTES

1. **NO HANDS** - Don't mention hands, gestures, touching, or holding
2. **NO EFFECTS** - Avoid words like: orb, glow, light, radiate, emanate, energy field
3. **FACE/HEAD ONLY** - Focus on eyes, brows, head movement, expression
4. **NATURAL** - Use conversational, human language not robotic direction
5. **SUBTLE** - Less is more. Small movements read better than big ones.
