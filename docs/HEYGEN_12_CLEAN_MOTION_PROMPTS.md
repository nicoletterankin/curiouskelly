# HeyGen: 12 Clean Kelly Motion Prompts

## 🎬 CUSTOM MOTION PROMPTS (PURE EXPRESSION)

Use these simplified prompts for the "No Props" archetypes. They focus strictly on facial expression and natural speaking to ensure maximum stability and class.

**Note:** Since the pose is locked, these prompts ensure the head stays centered while the face conveys the emotion.

---

### 1. THE SCIENTIST 🔬
`Subject speaks with calm, analytical confidence. Minimal head movement. Focus is on eyes which narrow slightly in thought. Eyebrow raises subtly to emphasize points. Mouth articulates clearly. Professional, intelligent energy.`

### 2. THE EXPLORER 🧭
`Subject speaks with bright enthusiasm. Eyes open wide with curiosity. Genuine smile while talking. Head stays centered but energy is lifted. Natural blinking. Engaging and happy.`

### 3. THE REBEL ⚡
`Subject speaks with cool, understated confidence. Direct eye contact. Subtle asymmetric smirk while talking. Chin stays level. Expression is challenging but composed. Minimal movement, maximum intensity.`

### 4. THE ARCHITECT 🏛️
`Subject speaks with methodical precision. Very steady head position. Calm, focused eyes. Expression is serious and thoughtful. Movements are slow and deliberate. Organizing thoughts while speaking.`

### 5. THE DIPLOMAT 🤝
`Subject speaks with warm, inviting openness. Gentle, friendly eye contact. Soft smile while talking. Head stays steady. Expression is balanced and trustworthy. soothing presence.`

### 6. THE EMPATH 💗
`Subject speaks with deep gentleness. Soft eyes, slight emotional warmth. Sincere expression. Very smooth mouth movements. Head stays centered. Radiates care and understanding.`

### 7. THE MACGYVER 🔧
`Subject speaks with bright, practical energy. Eyes spark with ideas. Quick, sharp smile. Alert and ready expression. Head remains stable. Energetic but grounded.`

### 8. THE MYSTIC ✨
`Subject speaks with serene, slow calmness. Eyes have a faraway, wise look. Gentle, peaceful smile. Very minimal movement. Ethereal and profound presence.`

### 9. THE PROVIDER 🛡️
`Subject speaks with steady, reassuring warmth. Solid, reliable gaze. Protective kindness in eyes. Calm, grounded expression. Head stays perfectly still. Comforting energy.`

### 10. THE STORYTELLER 📖
`Subject speaks with animated facial expression. Eyes light up. Eyebrows move to show wonder. engaging smile. Head stays centered but face is dynamic. Captivating and expressive.`

### 11. THE STRATEGIST 🎯
`Subject speaks with sharp, calculated focus. Intense eye contact. Confident, knowing expression. Minimal movement. Authority and intelligence in the face.`

### 12. THE SURVIVOR 🏕️
`Subject speaks with serious, determined grit. Steady, unblinking gaze. Set jaw while talking. Resilient and strong expression. No wasted movement.`
