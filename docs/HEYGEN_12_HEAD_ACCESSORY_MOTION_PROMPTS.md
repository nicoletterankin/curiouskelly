# HeyGen: 12 Kelly Head-Accessory Motion Prompts

## 🎬 CUSTOM MOTION PROMPTS (HEAD ACCESSORIES)

Use these simple prompts in HeyGen's "Custom prompt" field. They are optimized to keep head accessories stable while generating expressive talking motion.

---

### 1. THE SCIENTIST 🔬 (Goggles on forehead)
`Subject speaks with analytical confidence. Lab goggles on forehead remain stable. Deliberate hand gestures explaining data. Focused eye contact, occasional thoughtful eyebrow raise. Head moves naturally but keeps goggles steady.`

### 2. THE EXPLORER 🧭 (Aviator goggles + bandana)
`Infectious enthusiasm and wonder. Aviator goggles on head stay stable. Eyes wide with excitement. Animated gestures pointing out discoveries. Energetic head movements but keeping headgear secure. Genuine adventurous smile.`

### 3. THE REBEL ⚡ (Sunglasses in hair)
`Bold, challenging confidence. Sunglasses in hair remain stable. Direct unwavering eye contact. Knowing smirk. Head tilts with attitude. Assertive gestures. Chin down, looking through eyebrows. Edgy but approachable.`

### 4. THE ARCHITECT 🏛️ (Pencil + glasses)
`Calm, methodical precision. Pencil and glasses on head remain stable. Structured gestures building concepts in air. Deep concentration. Steady head position to keep accessories placed. Deliberate, purposeful movement.`

### 5. THE DIPLOMAT 🤝 (Pearl studs + headband)
`Warm, inclusive energy. Headband stays stable. Open welcoming gestures. Gentle understanding nods. Soft approachable eye contact. Slight forward lean showing interest. Balanced, graceful movements.`

### 6. THE EMPATH 💗 (Pink headband + lavender)
`Gentle, nurturing warmth. Headband remains stable. Soft caring eye contact. Hand occasionally touches heart. Compassionate head tilt. Smooth flowing movements. Leaning in to create connection.`

### 7. THE MACGYVER 🔧 (Shop glasses + bandana)
`Practical, hands-on energy. Bandana and glasses stay stable. Active gestures showing how things work. Bright eyes solving problems. Confident nods. Action-ready posture. Resourceful, can-do spirit.`

### 8. THE MYSTIC ✨ (Third eye gem + chain)
`Serene, profound calm. Forehead gem and chain remain perfectly stable. Slow graceful movements. Eyes with deep ancient knowing. Slight upward gaze. Peaceful smile. Minimal movement, maximum presence.`

### 9. THE PROVIDER 🛡️ (Knit headband)
`Steady, reassuring strength. Knit headband stays stable. Warm protective eye contact. Grounded posture. Hand on heart or open reassuring gestures. Reliable, maternal energy. Calm and dependable.`

### 10. THE STORYTELLER 📖 (Glasses on chain + feather)

### 11. THE STRATEGIST 🎯 (Angular glasses + chess clip)

### 12. THE SURVIVOR 🏕️ (Bandana + dog tags)
