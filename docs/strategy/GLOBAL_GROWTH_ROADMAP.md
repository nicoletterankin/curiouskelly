# 🌍 Global Growth Roadmap: 0 to 8 Billion (2026-2027)
**"The Universal Classroom" Strategy**

> **Objective:** Scale Curious Kelly / Lesson of the Day PBC from a US-centric launch to a planetary utility by EOY 2027.
> **Constraint:** "Radical Universalism" — One codebase, one lesson DNA, infinite cultural adaptations.

---

## 📅 Phase 1: The Anglosphere & Validation (Months 1-6)
**Territories:** USA, Canada, UK, Australia, New Zealand.  
**Target:** 1M Daily Active Learners (DAL).

### Readiness Standards (Gatekeepers)
- [x] **Content:** 365 Days of Golden V2 DNA (English).
- [x] **Voice:** Kelly/Kyle English Models (ElevenLabs) calibrated for 6 age personas.
- [ ] **Compliance:** COPPA (US), GDPR-UK, ACCC (Aus) alignment.
- [ ] **Infrastructure:** Edge caching in Virginia, London, Sydney.

### Execution Tactics
1.  **Trust & Safety Hardening:** Deploy the "Simulated Social Content" toggle (Completed Dec 2025) to satisfy Western digital wellbeing concerns.
2.  **Influencer & School Pilot:** Seed "Classroom Mode" in 500 US schools.
3.  **Billing:** Enable USD, GBP, CAD, AUD processing via Stripe.

### Key Metrics
*   **Retention:** >40% D30 (Day 30 retention).
*   **Completion:** >85% Lesson Completion Rate.
*   **CAC:** <$2.00 (Blended).

## 📅 Phase 2: The European Core & LatAm (Months 7-12)
**Territories:** EU (Germany, France, Spain), Latin America (Mexico, Brazil, Argentina).  
**Target:** 50M DAL.

### Readiness Standards (Gatekeepers)
- [ ] **Content:** 100% DNA Translation for ES, FR, DE, PT-BR. (No "Translation Pending" placeholders).
- [ ] **Voice:** Native-sounding TTS models for Euro/LatAm accents (not just translated text, but *culturally aligned* tone).
- [ ] **Compliance:** **GDPR (Strict Mode)**. Server-side data residency options for EU users.
- [ ] **Localization:** "PhaseDNA" cultural adaptation (e.g., swapping "baseball" metaphors for "football").

### Execution Tactics
1.  **The "Polyglot" Update:** Enable instantaneous language switching in the player (already architected).
2.  **Purchasing Power Parity (PPP):** Dynamic pricing for LatAm ($12.99 US -> $3.99 LatAm equivalent).
3.  **Government Partnerships:** Pitch "Lesson of the Day" as a supplementary curriculum to Ministries of Education.

---

## 📅 Phase 3: The Asian Velocity Layer (Months 13-18)
**Territories:** India, Japan, South Korea, Indonesia.  
**Target:** 250M DAL.

### Readiness Standards (Gatekeepers)
- [ ] **Content:** Hindi, Japanese, Korean, Bahasa.
- [ ] **Bandwidth:** "Low-Data Mode" (Audio-first fallback) for low-bandwidth regions in rural India/Indonesia.
- [ ] **Compliance:** India DPDP Act compliance; Korea KCC age verification.
- [ ] **Visuals:** Avatar distinctness (ensure Kelly's mannerisms read correctly across cultures, or introduce region-specific visual archetypes if validated).

### Execution Tactics
1.  **Mobile-First Optimization:** PWA (Progressive Web App) dominance. The Android app must be <20MB.
2.  **WhatsApp Integration:** Deliver lesson "hooks" via WhatsApp API (dominant platform in India).
3.  **The "Cram School" Alternative:** Position Kelly as the stress-free alternative to high-pressure Asian education systems.

---

## 📅 Phase 4: Global Access & The "Next Billion" (Months 19-24)
**Territories:** Africa (Nigeria, Kenya, SA), MENA (Egypt, UAE), Rest of World.  
**Target:** 1B+ DAL.

### Readiness Standards (Gatekeepers)
- [ ] **Content:** Arabic, Swahili, French (African dialects).
- [ ] **Access:** SMS-based delivery options (Text-only lessons).
- [ ] **Compliance:** Varied regional data laws.

### Execution Tactics
1.  **Carrier Partnerships:** Zero-rating data for the curiouskelly.com domain on major African networks (MTN, Vodafone).
2.  **Solar-Powered Kiosks:** Offline-first version for disconnected regions.
