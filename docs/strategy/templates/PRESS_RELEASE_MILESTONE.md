## Milestone Types & Hooks

Use these hooks for different milestone categories:

### Learner Volume Milestones
- "1 Million Lessons Taught"
- "Learners in 100 Countries"  
- "100,000 Daily Active Learners"

**Hook:** Scale proof—Kelly works at global scale

### Completion/Engagement Milestones
- "70% Completion Rate Achieved"
- "Average Streak Exceeds 30 Days"
- "1 Million Consecutive Learning Days"

**Hook:** Efficacy proof—Kelly actually creates habits (vs 5% MOOC completion)

### Age Diversity Milestones
- "Oldest Learner Turns 97"
- "Three Generations Learning Together"
- "Ages 2 to 97 Represented"

**Hook:** Lifelong learning proof—SDG 4 means ALL ages

### Equity Milestones
- "10,000 Scholarship Learners"
- "Launched in 10 New Languages"
- "Kelly Now Accessible Offline"

**Hook:** Access proof—education for those who couldn't afford/access otherwise

### Partnership Milestones
- "Partnership with UNESCO Announced"
- "Kelly Adopted by 500 Schools"
- "Fortune 500 Company Deploys Kelly for All Employees"

**Hook:** Institutional validation—credible organizations trust Kelly

---

## Usage Notes

1. Combine milestone with a human story—numbers + narrative
2. Always connect to SDG 4 framing
3. Use "learners" not "users"
4. Keep founder anonymous
5. Only use hello@curiouskelly.com
6. Include what's next to create momentum
