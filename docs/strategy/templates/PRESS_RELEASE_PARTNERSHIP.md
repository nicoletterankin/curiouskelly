## Partnership Categories & Framing

### Educational Institutions

**Examples:** UNESCO, school districts, universities, libraries  
**Frame:** "Bringing Kelly to learners where they already are"  
**Key metric:** Number of learners reached

### Corporate Partners

**Examples:** Fortune 500 employee programs, corporate foundations  
**Frame:** "Investing in lifelong learning for employees and communities"  
**Key metric:** Employee/family access, community funding

### Technology Partners

**Examples:** Device manufacturers, telecom companies, platform integrations  
**Frame:** "Making learning more accessible through technology"  
**Key metric:** New distribution channels, reduced access barriers

### Philanthropic Partners

**Examples:** Foundations, impact investors, government programs  
**Frame:** "Ensuring no one is left behind"  
**Key metric:** Scholarship learners funded, underserved populations reached

### Content Partners

**Examples:** National Geographic, museums, specialty content providers  
**Frame:** "Bringing world-class expertise to daily learning"  
**Key metric:** New curriculum content, expert credibility

5. Only use hello@curiouskelly.com for Curious Kelly
