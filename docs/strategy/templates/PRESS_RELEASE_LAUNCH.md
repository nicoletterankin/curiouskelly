CURIOUS KELLY LAUNCHES: THE WORLD'S FIRST AI TEACHER FOR AGES 2 TO 102

Founded by Open Education Co-Founder, Platform Aims to Complete the Mission 
of Free, Daily, Personalized Learning for Every Human Being

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

CALIFORNIA — December 17, 2025 — Lesson of the Day PBC today announced the 
public launch of Curious Kelly, an AI-powered educational platform that 
delivers personalized, age-adaptive lessons 365 days a year to learners of 
all ages—from toddlers taking their first steps in language to seniors 
mastering new technology.

Founded by a co-founder of the Open Education movement, Curious Kelly was 
built to complete what free educational resources alone could not achieve: 
the formation of daily learning habits through a trusted, personalized 
teacher-learner relationship.

"Open Education brought free resources to millions, but resources aren't 
enough," said the company. "People don't finish courses. They don't form 
habits. They don't have a teacher who knows their name. Kelly changes that."

KEY FEATURES:

• Age-Adaptive Learning: The same platform seamlessly adjusts for a 3-year-old 
  learning shapes or a 75-year-old exploring philosophy
  
• Voice-First Design: Accessible to pre-readers, visual impairments, and 
  anyone who prefers to listen and learn
  
• 365-Day Curriculum: A new lesson every day covering science, history, 
  philosophy, art, practical skills, and more
  
• Daily Habit Formation: Streaks, progress tracking, and gentle reminders 
  build consistent learning practice
  
• Multilingual: Available in English, Spanish, and French at launch, with 
  more languages planned

BUILT FOR SDG 4:

Curious Kelly directly addresses the United Nations Sustainable Development 
Goal 4: "Ensure inclusive and equitable quality education and promote 
lifelong learning opportunities for all."

"The world read 'all children' in SDG 4. We read 'all,'" the company stated. 
"Kelly teaches toddlers their first words and teaches seniors their first 
video calls. Education isn't preparation for life—it IS life."

PRICING & AVAILABILITY:

Curious Kelly is available now at www.curiouskelly.com with pricing starting 
at $199/year. A scholarship fund ensures that learners who cannot afford 
access can still participate.

ABOUT LESSON OF THE DAY PBC:

Lesson of the Day PBC is a California-based Public Benefit Corporation 
founded to provide lifelong learning opportunities to everyone. The company's 
mission is locked into its corporate structure: education for all, forever.
