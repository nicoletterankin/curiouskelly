**Deadline:** Usually February  
**Fee:** ~$400  
**Judging Criteria:** Innovation, impact, scalability, design  

**Our Angle:**
> Curious Kelly completes the Open Education mission—not just access to information, but access to a daily teacher who knows your name. By solving SDG 4 for everyone (ages 2-102), Kelly transforms education from a life stage into a life practice.

**Key Talking Points:**
- Founded by Open Education co-founder
- First AI teacher, not tutor (relationship vs transaction)
- 365 days, all ages, voice-first
- PBC structure = mission lock

## Award Team Responsibilities

### For Each Submission

1. **Research Phase** (2 weeks before deadline)
   - Download official guidelines
   - Review past winners
   - Identify exact category fit
   - Note word limits, video requirements, etc.

2. **Draft Phase** (1 week before deadline)
   - Write first draft of narrative
   - Gather metrics and data points
   - Collect testimonials/quotes
   - Create/update demo video

3. **Review Phase** (3 days before deadline)
   - External review for clarity
   - Fact-check all claims
   - Proofread for errors
   - Prepare payment

4. **Submit** (1 day before deadline)
   - Submit early to avoid technical issues
   - Screenshot confirmation
   - Add to tracking system
