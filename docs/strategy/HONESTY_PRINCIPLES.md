# HONESTY PRINCIPLES

> *"We believe Kelly deserves recognition—but we'll earn it through impact, not claims."*

---

## Core Principle

**We never claim what we haven't achieved.**

Philanthropic donors, award committees, and the press will eventually discover any exaggeration. Our credibility is our most valuable asset. Protect it fiercely.

---

## What We Can Say (Truthfully)

### ✅ Facts (Verified)
- "365 daily lessons" — True, curriculum is complete
- "Ages 2-102" — True, content is designed for all ages
- "3 languages at launch" — True, EN/ES/FR
- "Founded by a co-founder of Open Education" — True
- "Public Benefit Corporation" — True, legal structure
- "Built to solve SDG 4" — True, that's our mission
- "$199/year pricing" — True, current price
- "Scholarship fund available" — True, if it's set up

### ⚠️ Goals (Clearly Labeled)
- "Goal: 10M lessons by 2026" — Aspirational, clearly marked
- "Target: TIME recognition" — Aspirational, clearly marked
- "We hope to tell stories like..." — Aspirational, clearly marked
- "Awards we're pursuing" — Not wins, just targets

### ❌ Never Say
- "X million learners" — Unless we have them
- "Award winner" — Unless we've won
- "As featured in [Publication]" — Unless actually featured
- "70% completion rate" — Unless measured
- "[X] countries reached" — Unless we have data

---

## Press Release Rules

1. **Only announce what's real** — Don't pre-announce milestones
2. **Use "goal" language for aspirations** — "Our goal is..." not "We have..."
3. **Cite sources for any statistics** — If we claim a number, we need proof
4. **Date everything** — So context is clear

---

## Website Audit Checklist

Before any deployment, verify:

- [ ] No fake learner counts
- [ ] No fake lesson counts (unless from real database)
- [ ] No awards listed as "won" that weren't won
- [ ] No testimonials/stories presented as real that are aspirational
- [ ] No statistics without sources
- [ ] Correct email (hello@curiouskelly.com ONLY)
- [ ] Correct legal entity (Lesson of the Day PBC)
- [ ] Goals clearly marked as goals

---

## Why This Matters

1. **Philanthropic donors do due diligence** — They will check claims
2. **Award committees verify submissions** — Exaggeration disqualifies
3. **Press fact-checks** — Getting caught lying destroys credibility forever
4. **It's just right** — Kelly is about education, and honesty is educational

---

## The Trust Equation

```
TRUST = (Credibility + Reliability + Intimacy) / Self-Orientation
```

- **Credibility**: Do we tell the truth?
- **Reliability**: Do we do what we say?
- **Intimacy**: Do people feel safe with us?
- **Self-Orientation**: Are we focused on ourselves or the learner?

Exaggeration destroys all four.

---

## Recovery Protocol

If we discover we've made a false claim:

1. **Fix immediately** — Remove or correct the claim
2. **Document** — Note what was wrong and when fixed
3. **Notify if necessary** — If claim was in press release or award submission
4. **Learn** — Update processes to prevent recurrence

## Aspirational Language Templates

### For metrics we don't have yet:
> "Our goal is [X]. We'll share real metrics as we grow."

### For stories we hope to tell:
> "These are the kinds of stories we're building toward—real impact we hope to measure and share."

### For awards we're pursuing:
> "Awards we're actively working toward in 2026."

### For geographic reach:
> "Available worldwide. We'll track and share our geographic reach as we grow."
