## 💡 **KEY INSIGHTS**

### **What We Learned:**

1. **Visibility = Usage** - Hidden features don't get used, no matter how good they are
2. **Spatial Consistency > Feature Count** - Better to have 20 features with clear homes than 50 hidden ones
3. **Progressive Disclosure Works** - Tier 1 (always visible) → Tier 2 (1 tap) → Tier 3 (2 taps) → Tier 4 (background)
4. **Nothing Removed** - Every feature from 20 years preserved and honored
5. **Mission Control = Proof** - Show everything at once to prove nothing is hidden

1. **20 Years of Work** - 47 features, all preserved

3. **Progressive Disclosure** - Simple by default, powerful when needed

5. **Zero Removals** - Nothing deleted, everything organized
