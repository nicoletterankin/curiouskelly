# HeyGen: 12 Shadow Kelly Motion Prompts

## 🌚 CUSTOM MOTION PROMPTS (SHADOW SET)

Use these prompts for the "Shadow" archetypes. They are designed to convey specific psychological states while maintaining the "locked pose" consistency.

---

### 1. THE CLOWN 🤡 (Distraction)
`Subject speaks with exaggerated, playful energy. Eyes go wide and goofy. Eyebrows raise high often. Big, silly smile while talking. Head bobs slightly with enthusiasm. Entertaining but distracting.`

### 2. THE VICTIM 🥺 (Helplessness)
`Subject speaks with sad, pleading energy. Eyes look slightly upward (puppy dog eyes). Eyebrows furrowed in worry. Mouth corners downturned even while speaking. Head tilts vulnerably. Radiates helplessness.`

### 3. THE BULLY 😈 (Aggression)
`Subject speaks with sneering, aggressive confidence. Eyes narrow cruelly. Chin lifts slightly in arrogance. Mouth moves with sharp, biting articulation. Intense, intimidating eye contact. Dominating energy.`

### 4. THE JUDGE ⚖️ (Criticism)
`Subject speaks with stern disapproval. Lips pursed tight between words. Eyes narrow critically. Head remains very still and rigid. Cold, judgmental stare. Radiates disappointment.`

### 5. THE PERFECTIONIST 📝 (Anxiety)
`Subject speaks with tense, anxious energy. Eyes dart slightly as if checking for errors. Tight, strained smile. Fast blinking. Head movements are stiff and controlled. Radiates high-strung stress.`

### 6. THE PEOPLE PLEASER 🤝 (Fawning)
`Subject speaks with overly eager, desperate warmth. Nodding constantly while talking. Wide, pleading smile. Eyes seek approval. Head tilts submissively. Radiates 'please like me' energy.`

### 7. THE SKEPTIC 🤨 (Doubt)
`Subject speaks with obvious disbelief. One eyebrow raised high. Mouth pulls to one side skeptically. Eyes squint in doubt. Head tilts away slightly. Radiates 'I don't believe you'.`

### 8. THE PESSIMIST ☁️ (Gloom)
`Subject speaks with low, heavy energy. Eyelids droop. Mouth corners sag. Slow, resigned head movements. Sighing expression. Radiates defeat and gloom.`

### 9. THE DIVA 💅 (Entitlement)
`Subject speaks with haughty superiority. Chin lifted high. Looking down her nose at the viewer. Bored, expectant eyes. Slow, deliberate blinks. Radiates 'I am better than this'.`

### 10. THE ROBOT 🤖 (Numbness)
`Subject speaks with zero emotion. Face is completely blank. Eyes are dead and unblinking. Mouth moves mechanically. Head is perfectly still. Uncanny valley lack of affect.`

### 11. THE GHOST 👻 (Fear)
`Subject speaks with shocked, fearful energy. Eyes wide and staring. Face pale and tense. Shallow breathing visible. Frozen expression of fright. Radiates 'something is wrong'.`

### 12. THE SABOTEUR 🗡️ (Mischief)
`Subject speaks with cunning, secretive energy. Eyes look sideways conspiratorially. Sly, tricky smirk. Head lowers slightly. Radiates 'I'm up to no good'.`
