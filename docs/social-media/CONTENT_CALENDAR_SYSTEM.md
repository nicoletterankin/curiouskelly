## 📅 Content Calendar Philosophy

### The 80/20 Rule
- **80% Educational/Entertaining:** Value-first content that teaches, inspires, or entertains
- **20% Promotional:** Product features, CTAs, conversion-focused content

### The 4 Content Pillars

Every post should fall into one of these categories:

#### 1. **EDUCATE** (40%)
*Teach something valuable*
- Daily facts
- Mini-lessons
- How-to guides
- Explainer threads
- Lesson previews

#### 2. **INSPIRE** (25%)
*Make people feel something*
- Motivational quotes
- Success stories
- Learning journey spotlights
- Wisdom moments
- Community wins

#### 3. **ENGAGE** (25%)
*Start conversations*
- Questions & polls
- Behind-the-scenes
- Challenges
- User-generated content features
- Interactive content

#### 4. **CONVERT** (10%)
*Drive action*
- Product announcements
- Feature highlights
- Testimonials
- Limited offers
- Call-to-action posts

## 📊 Weekly Posting Schedule (Per Platform)

### Twitter / X (21 posts/week)
- **Daily:** 3 posts minimum
  - Morning (7-9 AM): Motivational/Question
  - Midday (12-1 PM): Educational fact/thread
  - Evening (7-8 PM): Engagement/Tomorrow's lesson tease
- **Weekly:** 1 long thread (educational deep-dive)

### Instagram (14 posts/week)
- **Daily:** 1 feed post + 3-5 Stories
  - Feed post (10 AM): High-quality, polished content
  - Stories (throughout day): Raw, spontaneous, behind-scenes
- **Weekly:** 3-4 Reels (M/W/F/Su)

### YouTube (2 videos/week)
- **Tuesday:** Lesson preview or educational content
- **Friday:** Behind-the-scenes or community Q&A

### LinkedIn (3 posts/week)
- **Monday:** Motivational/Thought leadership
- **Wednesday:** Educational article or industry insight
- **Friday:** Company update or customer success story

### TikTok (10-14 posts/week)
- **Daily:** 1-2 short videos
  - Best times: 9 AM, 12 PM, 7 PM (ET)
- Focus: Viral-worthy, trend participation, educational entertainment

### Discord (Daily)
- **Every morning (8 AM PT):** Auto-post today's lesson topic
- **Ongoing:** Monitor and engage in discussions
- **Weekly (Wednesdays, 5 PM PT):** "Kelly's Office Hours" live Q&A
- **Monthly:** Community challenge announcement

## 📝 Content Creation Workflow

### Monthly Planning (Last week of previous month)

**Step 1: Content Brainstorm** (2 hours)
- Review last month's analytics (what performed best?)
- Identify upcoming events, holidays, awareness days
- List lesson topics for the month
- Brainstorm viral-worthy ideas
- Check competitor/influencer trends

**Step 2: Content Calendar Population** (3 hours)
- Use template (see below)
- Assign themes to each day
- Balance content pillars (40/25/25/10)
- Ensure variety (text, image, video, carousel)
- Mark priority posts (launches, announcements)

**Step 3: Asset List** (1 hour)
- List all graphics needed
- List all videos to record
- Identify UGC to request
- Note stock footage/images needed

---

### Weekly Workflow (Every Monday)

**Step 1: Review & Adjust** (30 min)
- Check content calendar for this week
- Adjust for any breaking news or trends
- Confirm all assets are ready
- Review last week's performance

**Step 2: Batch Creation Day** (4-6 hours)
- Write all copy for the week
- Design all graphics (Canva batch mode)
- Record videos (set up once, record 3-5)
- Download/organize UGC

**Step 3: Scheduling** (1 hour)
- Upload to Buffer/Hootsuite
- Double-check dates, times, links
- Set up first-comment hashtags (Instagram)
- Schedule Stories (Later app)

**Step 4: Approval** (30 min, if team)
- Share preview with team lead
- Make any revisions
- Get final approval
- Mark as "Scheduled" in calendar

---

### Daily Tasks (30-60 minutes)

**Morning (9-10 AM):**
- [ ] Check scheduled posts published correctly
- [ ] Monitor overnight comments/mentions
- [ ] Respond to all comments from last 24 hours
- [ ] Check Discord, post daily topic discussion

**Midday (12-1 PM):**
- [ ] Engage with community (like, comment on others' posts)
- [ ] Monitor real-time mentions
- [ ] Capture any spontaneous content (Stories)

**Evening (6-7 PM):**
- [ ] Final comment check and replies
- [ ] Screenshot any notable moments (high engagement, testimonials)
- [ ] Post spontaneous content if relevant
- [ ] Review next day's scheduled content

## 🎯 Special Event Content Planning

### Product Launches
**Timeline:**
- T-7 days: Teaser post
- T-3 days: Countdown begins
- T-1 day: "Tomorrow!" hype
- Launch day: 3-5 posts across platforms
- T+1 day: Recap, testimonials
- T+7 days: Results, analytics

### Holidays / Awareness Days
**Planning:**
- Mark on calendar 30 days prior
- Brainstorm relevant angle (how does it connect to learning?)
- Create special content (not just generic "Happy [Holiday]")
- Engage with community around shared experience

**Examples:**
- **World Curiosity Day (Jun 4):** Major campaign
- **Teachers Day (Oct 5):** Teacher appreciation, educator content
- **International Literacy Day (Sep 8):** Reading, language lessons
- **Pi Day (Mar 14):** Math lessons, pi facts
- **Earth Day (Apr 22):** Environmental science lessons

### Milestones
**When you hit these, celebrate!**
- Follower counts (100, 1K, 10K, 100K)
- Subscriber counts (100, 1K, 10K)
- App downloads
- Lesson completion stats
- Community achievements
- Press features

## 🔄 Content Repurposing Strategy

### Don't Create New Content—Remix It!

**1 YouTube Video = 10+ Pieces of Content**

Example: "How Curious Kelly Works" (8-minute YouTube video)

**Repurpose into:**
1. Twitter thread (key points)
2. Instagram carousel (visuals from video)
3. TikTok clips (3x 30-second segments)
4. LinkedIn article (long-form text version)
5. Blog post (with video embedded)
6. Instagram Reels (3x different angles)
7. Quote graphics (pull 5 quotes from video)
8. Discord discussion topic
9. Email newsletter content
10. Podcast episode (audio version)

**Batch Content Creation:**
- Record 5 videos in one session → 50+ pieces of content
- Design 10 graphics with variations → 30+ posts
- Write 5 long threads → 20+ quote cards

## 📈 Content Performance Review

### Weekly Review (Every Monday, 30 minutes)

**Questions to answer:**
1. What was our best-performing post? Why?
2. What flopped? Why?
3. Which platform drove the most traffic?
4. What content type got the most engagement?
5. Any surprising trends or patterns?

**Actions:**
- Double down on what works
- Eliminate what doesn't
- Test new hypotheses
- Adjust next week's content

### Monthly Review (Last Friday of month, 2 hours)

**Comprehensive analysis:**
- Top 10 posts (by engagement, reach, conversions)
- Platform comparison (which is growing fastest?)
- Content pillar balance (are we too promotional?)
- Audience insights (demographics, behavior)
- Competitor analysis (what are others doing?)

**Strategic adjustments:**
- Update content calendar template
- Refresh visual templates
- Test new content formats
- Pivot platform focus if needed

## ✅ Content Calendar Checklist

**Before scheduling any post:**
- [ ] Aligned with content pillar and theme
- [ ] Provides value (educate, inspire, or engage)
- [ ] CTA is clear
- [ ] Visual is high-quality and on-brand
- [ ] Copy is proofread (no typos!)
- [ ] Hashtags are relevant
- [ ] Link works (if included)
- [ ] Posting time is optimal
- [ ] Fits into overall content mix (not too many promos in a row)

## 🎓 Content Calendar Best Practices

1. **Plan ahead, but stay flexible:** 80% scheduled, 20% spontaneous
2. **Batch create:** Set aside dedicated time, be efficient
3. **Repurpose ruthlessly:** One piece of content = many formats
4. **Track everything:** You can't improve what you don't measure
5. **Listen to your audience:** Let data guide your decisions
6. **Stay consistent:** Regular posting > occasional viral posts
7. **Quality over quantity:** One great post > five mediocre posts
8. **Experiment constantly:** Test new formats, times, platforms
9. **Collaborate:** Involve team, ask for feedback
10. **Enjoy the process:** If you're bored creating it, your audience will be bored consuming it
