## 📊 Strategic Framework

### The 4 Content Pillars

| Pillar | % of Content | Purpose |
|--------|--------------|---------|
| **EDUCATE** | 40% | Teach something valuable (facts, how-tos, lessons) |
| **INSPIRE** | 25% | Make people feel (quotes, stories, wisdom) |
| **ENGAGE** | 25% | Start conversations (questions, polls, UGC) |
| **CONVERT** | 10% | Drive action (testimonials, CTAs, offers) |

### Weekly Posting Schedule

| Platform | Daily Posts | Weekly Total | Time Investment |
|----------|-------------|--------------|-----------------|
| Twitter | 3-5 | 21-35 | 30 min/day |
| Instagram | 1 feed + 3-5 Stories | 10-14 | 45 min/day |
| TikTok | 1-2 videos | 7-14 | 1 hour/day |
| YouTube | - | 2 videos | 4 hours/week |
| LinkedIn | - | 2-3 posts | 1 hour/week |
| Discord | Daily moderation | - | 30 min/day |

**Total time investment:** ~3-4 hours/day with automation  
**Without automation:** ~6-8 hours/day
