### 3. Thread Starter (Educational Deep-Dive)
```
Let's talk about [topic] 🧵👇

[Hook/surprising fact]

(1/[X])
```

**Thread Structure:**
1. Hook (surprising fact or question)
2. Context (why this matters)
3. Explanation (break it down)
4. Real-world example
5. Interesting detail or twist
6. Key takeaway
7. Call-to-action

**Example:**
```
Tweet 1: Let's talk about why the sky is blue 🧵👇

It's not because of the ocean (common myth). The answer is cooler than that.

(1/6)

Tweet 2: Sunlight is made of all colors mixed together—what we see as white light.

But different colors have different wavelengths.

(2/6)

Tweet 3: Blue light has shorter wavelengths than red light.

When sunlight hits Earth's atmosphere, it collides with tiny gas molecules.

(3/6)

Tweet 4: These collisions scatter the light in all directions.

Blue light scatters more because of its shorter wavelength—up to 10x more than red!

(4/6)

Tweet 5: So when you look up, you see blue light scattered from all over the sky.

At sunrise/sunset, light travels through more atmosphere, scattering away the blue and leaving red/orange.

(5/6)

Tweet 6: Science of everyday beauty. 🌅

Want to learn something new every day? That's what I do at Curious Kelly.

8 minutes a day. Ages 2-102.

Start free: [link]

(6/6)
```

## 🎨 Visual Templates (Design Guidelines)

### Canva Template Structure

**Template 1: Fact Post**
- Dimensions: 1080 x 1080px (square)
- Background: Dark gradient (#0f0f11 to #18181b)
- Kelly's Avatar: Left 35%, slightly overlapping border
- Main Text: Times New Roman Bold, 48-64pt, white
- Body Text: SF Pro Regular, 18-24pt, #a1a1aa
- Accent: Orange line or highlight (#d97757)
- Logo: Bottom right, 80px width

**Template 2: Quote Post**
- Dimensions: 1080 x 1350px (portrait)
- Background: Solid dark #0f0f11
- Quote Text: Times New Roman Bold, 56-72pt, centered, white
- Attribution: SF Pro Medium, 18pt, #a1a1aa, centered
- Orange quotation marks: 120pt, top-left and bottom-right
- Kelly's avatar: Subtle, 20% opacity in background
- Logo: Bottom center

**Template 3: Story Template**
- Dimensions: 1080 x 1920px (9:16)
- Safe areas: Keep important content in middle 1080 x 1440px
- Background: Gradient or solid
- Text: Large, bold, high contrast
- Stickers: Top and bottom thirds
- CTA: Bottom 300px

## 📋 Content Template Best Practices

1. **Customize, don't copy-paste:** Templates are starting points. Add your unique angle.
2. **Update regularly:** Refresh templates every quarter to stay fresh.
3. **A/B test variations:** Try different hooks, CTAs, formats.
4. **Save what works:** When a post performs well, save it as a template.
5. **Brand consistency:** All templates should feel like Curious Kelly.
6. **Accessibility first:** Ensure alt text, captions, high contrast.
7. **Mobile-first:** 80% of social media is consumed on mobile. Design for small screens.

---

## 🔄 Template Workflow

### When creating a new post:

1. **Choose template** that matches content type
2. **Copy template** to your content doc
3. **Customize** with specific topic/fact/hook
4. **Add visual** (create in Canva from template or commission)
5. **Proofread** carefully (typos kill credibility)
6. **Get feedback** (team review if available)
7. **Schedule** in Buffer/Hootsuite
8. **Track performance** (add to analytics sheet)
9. **Iterate** (adjust template based on results)
