## Official Logo

```
✨ Curious Kelly
```

**Symbol:** ✨ (Sparkles emoji/unicode U+2728)  
**Wordmark:** Curious Kelly  
**Primary Color:** #2563eb (Kelly Blue)

---

## Why Sparkles?

### 1. **Embodies Curiosity**
The sparkles symbol perfectly captures wonder, discovery, and the "aha!" moments of learning. It's the visual representation of curiosity itself.

### 2. **Related to Claude, But Distinct**
- Claude uses: ✴ (starburst)
- Kelly uses: ✨ (sparkles)
- Sister brands, not twins. Similar energy, different personality.

### 3. **Social Media Friendly**
- Native emoji support everywhere (Twitter, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, Discord, YouTube)
- No custom font files needed
- Renders consistently across platforms
- Works in text, HTML, and native apps

### 4. **Warm & Approachable**
Unlike technical/corporate symbols, sparkles feels:
- Magical (learning is magical)
- Warm (Kelly is a warm teacher)
- Positive (celebrates discovery)
- Playful yet professional

### 5. **Scalability**
- Works at any size (12px to 512px)
- Clear at profile pic sizes
- Distinctive in headers
- Recognizable as icon-only

## Technical Specifications

### Unicode
- Character: ✨
- Unicode: U+2728
- HTML Entity: `&#10024;`
- CSS: `\2728`
- Name: SPARKLES

### Colors
- Primary: `#2563eb` (Kelly Blue)
- On dark: `#2563eb`
- On light: `#1d4ed8` (slightly darker for contrast)
- Monochrome: `#f4f4f5` (white) or `#18181b` (dark)

### Typography
- Font: System UI / San Francisco / Segoe UI
- Weight: 300 (light) for "Curious Kelly"
- Letter-spacing: -0.5px (tight)
- Size ratio: Icon 1.3x larger than text

## Usage Guidelines

### ✅ DO
- Use ✨ symbol consistently across all materials
- Maintain color (#d97757) when possible
- Use white/monochrome versions on colored backgrounds
- Keep adequate whitespace around logo (minimum 20% of logo height)
- Use system fonts (no custom fonts required)

### ❌ DON'T
- Replace sparkles with other symbols
- Use starburst (✴) - that's Claude's
- Distort proportions
- Add effects (shadows, glows, outlines) unless brand-approved
- Use on busy backgrounds without sufficient contrast
- Mix sparkles with other emoji/symbols

## Decision Rationale

Evaluated 9 logo concepts against 4 criteria (Curiosity, Learning, Social Media, Uniqueness):

| Concept | Curiosity | Learning | Social | Unique | Total |
|---------|-----------|----------|--------|--------|-------|
| **✨ Sparkles** | 10 | 8 | 10 | 9 | **37** ✓ |
| 💡 Lightbulb | 7 | 10 | 9 | 6 | 32 |
| ? Question | 10 | 7 | 8 | 7 | 32 |
| ✴ Starburst | 6 | 5 | 9 | 6 | 26 |
| 📖 Book | 5 | 9 | 7 | 4 | 25 |

Sparkles won decisively on curiosity + social media + uniqueness while maintaining strong learning association.
