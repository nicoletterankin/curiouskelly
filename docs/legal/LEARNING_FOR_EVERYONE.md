## In Brief

> Curious Kelly exists because curiosity knows no boundaries—but access often does. We built a platform where every learner can learn, every learner can earn, and every learner can build. That is not a slogan. It is the architecture.

## The Three Pillars

At Lesson of the Day PBC, we believe learning should flow like water: freely, naturally, to everyone who thirsts.

Curious Kelly is built on three pillars:

### Learn

Every learner deserves Kelly's full attention. Lessons adapt to your age, your pace, and your understanding. No one is left behind because they learn differently.

### Earn

Every learner is an affiliate from Day 1. When you share Curious Kelly and someone subscribes, you earn—forever. The more you learn, the more you earn. This is not a rewards program. It is a redistribution of value.

### Build

Every learner can improve the curriculum. Through the **Learner Commons**, you propose changes, vote on improvements, and contribute knowledge that makes Kelly better for everyone who comes after you. Through **BYOK (Bring Your Own Key)**, you can contribute your unused API credits to generate content that benefits the entire community.

## Accessibility

### Cognitive Accessibility

Learning differences are not deficits—they are variations. Curious Kelly supports multiple ways of understanding:

- **Visual learners:** Rich infographics, animations, and diagrams
- **Auditory learners:** Kelly's voice narrates every concept
- **Kinesthetic learners:** Interactive choices and hands-on exercises
- **Reading-based learners:** Full transcripts and written summaries

### Physical Accessibility

We design for full accessibility compliance:

- **Screen reader compatibility:** Semantic HTML, ARIA labels, proper heading structure
- **Keyboard navigation:** Every feature accessible without a mouse
- **Motor accessibility:** Large click targets, no timed interactions
- **High contrast mode:** For low vision users
- **Reduced motion:** Respects prefers-reduced-motion

Our target is **WCAG 2.1 Level AA** compliance, with aspirations for AAA where feasible.

### Sensory Accessibility

- **Closed captions:** On all video content
- **Audio descriptions:** For visual content (in development)
- **Adjustable playback speed:** 0.5x to 2x
- **Text resize:** Without breaking layout

## Neurodiversity

We celebrate the full spectrum of how minds work:

### ADHD-Friendly Design

- **Micro-sessions:** 5-10 minute lessons with clear start/stop points
- **Gamification without addiction:** Progress tracking without manipulative mechanics
- **Reduced cognitive load:** One concept at a time, clear visual hierarchy
- **Flexible scheduling:** Resume exactly where you left off

### Autism-Friendly Design

- **Predictable structure:** Every lesson follows the same flow
- **Explicit communication:** Kelly says exactly what she means
- **Sensory options:** Control over sound, motion, and visual intensity
- **Special interests:** Deep-dive content for those who want more

### Dyslexia-Friendly Design

- **Clean typography:** Sans-serif fonts, generous spacing
- **Audio accompaniment:** Never rely on reading alone
- **Chunked content:** Short paragraphs, clear sections
- **Visual structure:** Icons and colors to support navigation

## Economic Accessibility

Education should not be a luxury. We are building toward universal access:

### The Affiliate Model

Traditional education extracts money from learners. We reverse the flow:

- Every learner is an affiliate from Day 1
- Commission rates increase with learning progress (10% → 35%)
- Lifetime attribution means you earn on referrals forever
- No complicated rules, no expiring cookies, no fine print

This is not a side feature. It is the business model. Learners who cannot afford to pay can earn their way to access—and beyond.

### BYOK (Bring Your Own Key)

Many learners have unused API credits (from OpenAI, ElevenLabs, Google AI, and others). BYOK lets them contribute those credits to generate content for Curious Kelly.

When you generate a visual, a voice clip, or any asset with your key:

- Your key stays private (never stored on our servers)
- The output is cached and shared with everyone
- You are credited as a contributor
- The community grows stronger

This is collective resource pooling. Everyone with extra contributes. Everyone with less receives. The curriculum improves continuously.

### Scholarship Goals

As we grow, we commit to:

- Sliding-scale pricing based on economic need
- Sponsored access for low-income learners
- Partnership with schools, libraries, and community organizations
- A path to make the first lesson of every day available at no cost to all

## The Learner Commons

The Learner Commons is how we stay honest.

### What It Is

A community-governed knowledge base where learners can:

- **Vote** on proposed lesson changes
- **Propose** enhancements, corrections, or new content
- **Discuss** teaching approaches and perspectives
- **Track** the status of community suggestions
- **Celebrate** when their contributions ship

### Why It Matters

Traditional curricula are static. They reflect the perspectives of their authors at a single moment in time. Knowledge evolves. Understanding deepens. Mistakes surface.

The Learner Commons ensures that every lesson gets better, year after year. If Kelly says something wrong, you can fix it. If a lesson lacks context, you can add it. If an explanation doesn't land, you can propose a better one.

### Recognition

Contributors are recognized by level:

| Level | Requirements | Badge |
|-------|--------------|-------|
| Newcomer | Just starting | — |
| Contributor | 5+ accepted contributions | 🌱 |
| Trusted | 25+ accepted contributions | 🌿 |
| Expert | 100+ accepted contributions | 🌳 |
| Steward | Community moderator | 👑 |

### Moderation

All contributions are moderated for accuracy, safety, and inclusivity. Community guidelines require being constructive, specific, respectful, factual, and inclusive.

## Reporting & Accountability

### How to Report

If you encounter accessibility barriers, content issues, or any concern:

- **Email:** [hello@curiouskelly.com](mailto:hello@curiouskelly.com)
- **In-App:** Use the "Suggest Improvement" button on any lesson
- **Learner Commons:** Submit a formal proposal for community review

### Our Commitment

We respond to accessibility reports within 48 hours and resolve critical barriers within 7 days.

### Transparency

We publish annual accessibility and inclusion reports (coming 2026) documenting:

- Accessibility audit results
- Learner Commons activity and implementation rates
- Demographic diversity of our learner base (anonymized)
- Economic impact of the affiliate program
