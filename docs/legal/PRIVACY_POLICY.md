**Last Updated:** December 1, 2025  
**Effective Date:** December 1, 2025

## In Brief

> We collect only what we need to help you learn. Your learning data stays yours. We never sell your information. Children under 13 cannot create accounts. Your privacy is not a product—it is a right.

---

## 1. Who We Are

At Lesson of the Day PBC, we believe privacy is a fundamental right—especially for learners. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and share information when you use Curious Kelly, our educational platform.

Lesson of the Day PBC is a California Public Benefit Corporation. When this Policy says "we," "us," or "our," we mean Lesson of the Day PBC. When it says "you" or "your," we mean you—the learner.

## 2. Information We Collect

### Information You Provide

| Category | Examples | Why We Need It |
|----------|----------|----------------|
| **Account Information** | Email, name, age range, password | To create and secure your account |
| **Profile Data** | Display name, avatar preferences | To personalize your experience |
| **Payment Information** | Card details (via Stripe) | To process subscriptions |
| **Learning Responses** | Quiz answers, lesson choices, progress | To adapt lessons to your understanding |

| Category | Examples | Why We Need It |
|----------|----------|----------------|

| **Usage Data** | Lessons viewed, time spent, completion rates | To understand learning patterns |

- Using testimonials or stories you submit
- Enabling third-party integrations you request

## 5. How We Share Information

### We NEVER:

- Sell your personal information
- Share learning data with advertisers
- Allow data brokers to access our systems
- Share individual learning performance without consent

### We May Share:

| Recipient | What We Share | Why |
|-----------|---------------|-----|
| **Stripe** | Payment information | To process payments securely |
| **Supabase** | Account and learning data | Secure database hosting |
| **Analytics Providers** | Aggregate, anonymized data | To understand usage patterns |
| **Legal Authorities** | Required information | When legally compelled |
| **Successor Entity** | All data (with notice) | In case of merger or acquisition |

### Learner Commons & BYOK

When you contribute to the Learner Commons, your proposals, notes, and votes may be visible to other learners (with your display name or anonymously, per your preference).

When you use BYOK (Bring Your Own Key) to generate content, the output may be cached and shared—but your API key is NEVER shared or stored on our servers.

### Affiliate Program

To process your affiliate earnings, we share necessary information with Stripe (our payment processor). We display your referral statistics in your dashboard but never expose your referees' personal information to you beyond aggregate counts.

## 8. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

### Under 13

Users under 13 may NOT create accounts. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If we discover a user is under 13, we will immediately delete their account and data. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us immediately at [hello@curiouskelly.com](mailto:hello@curiouskelly.com).

### Ages 13-17

Teenagers 13-17 may create accounts with parent/guardian consent. We recommend parents review these Terms and this Policy with their teens.

We are developing Family Accounts that will allow parents to manage learning for children of all ages while maintaining COPPA compliance.

## 10. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

- **Encryption:** TLS 1.3 in transit, AES-256 at rest
- **Authentication:** Secure password hashing, optional 2FA
- **Access Control:** Role-based access, audit logging
- **Monitoring:** 24/7 threat detection
- **Vendor Security:** SOC 2 certified partners
- **BYOK Safety:** Your API keys are stored locally in your browser—never on our servers

No system is perfectly secure. If you discover a vulnerability, please report it to [hello@curiouskelly.com](mailto:hello@curiouskelly.com).
