# Legal Documents

> *Learn. Earn. Build.*

This folder contains the legal foundation of Curious Kelly—the policies and promises that govern how we operate, how we protect learners, and how we build together.

---

## The Three Pillars

Every document in this folder reflects three core principles:

### 🎓 Learn
Every learner deserves personalized, accessible, joyful education. Our Terms protect your right to learn. Our Privacy Policy ensures your learning data serves you—not advertisers.

### 💰 Earn
Every learner is an affiliate from Day 1. Our Terms explain how the program works—lifetime attribution, progressive commission rates, and the philosophy that learners should share in the value they create.

### 🔨 Build
Every learner can improve the curriculum. Our Terms establish the Learner Commons and BYOK systems. Our Learning for Everyone document explains why collective contribution makes education better for all.

## Key Concepts

### Learner Commons
A community-governed knowledge base where learners propose, vote on, and contribute improvements to lessons. Every lesson gets better year after year through collective intelligence.

### BYOK (Bring Your Own Key)
Learners can contribute their unused API credits to generate content for Curious Kelly. Your key stays private; the output benefits everyone. Collective resource pooling at its finest.

### Lifetime Attribution
When someone clicks your affiliate link, they are attributed to you permanently. No 30-day windows. No expiring cookies. If they subscribe years later, you still get credited.

### Progressive Commissions
Your affiliate commission rate increases with your learning progress—from 10% (new learner) to 35% (legendary learner with 1000+ lessons). Learning unlocks earning potential.
