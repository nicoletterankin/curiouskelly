## 🔴 CRITICAL FINANCIAL RISKS

### 1. Payment Processing
- **Risk:** Stripe account could be flagged as high-risk (subscription + trial model)
- **Mitigation:** Pre-warm Stripe with documentation; maintain <1% dispute rate

### 2. Trial Abuse
- **Risk:** Users cycling free trials with different emails
- **Mitigation:** Implement device fingerprinting; limit trial to new payment methods

### 3. Holiday Gift Revenue Timing
- **Risk:** Gift revenue is one-time; January could show steep MRR drop
- **Mitigation:** Push annual plans; create January retention campaign

### 4. Affiliate Fraud
- **Risk:** Self-referral schemes; fake conversions
- **Mitigation:** 30-day cookie; payment verification; manual review >$500 payout

### 5. Currency/Tax Complexity
- **Risk:** EU VAT MOSS; state sales tax nexus
- **Mitigation:** Use Stripe Tax; limit initial launch to US/CA
