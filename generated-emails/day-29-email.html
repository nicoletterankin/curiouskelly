⏳ Why Time Feels Different

Time is constant, but our experience of it expands and contracts based on attention and novelty

Remember how summer vacation felt endless as a child? Now years vanish in a blur. Time hasn't changed—something in you has.

💡 What you'll discover:

  • Time seems to speed up as we age because fewer experiences are novel enough to form distinct memories
  • Fear stretches time—the brain processes more information during threatening situations
  • The 'watched pot' effect is real: focusing on time passing makes it feel slower

Five minutes. I think you'll love it.
