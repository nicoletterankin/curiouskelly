🏃 Why Bodies Need to Move

Your body was designed to move—sitting is the new smoking

For millions of years, humans moved constantly—walking, running, climbing, building. Now we sit for an average of 10 hours a day.

💡 What you'll discover:

  • A 20-minute walk can boost creative thinking by up to 60%
  • Prolonged sitting increases mortality risk even for people who exercise regularly
  • Your brain gets 15% more blood flow when you're walking than when you're sitting

Five minutes. I think you'll love it.

🏃 Today's Growth Challenge
