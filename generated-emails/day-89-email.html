🦴 Your Body's Framework

Your 206 bones are living organs that grow, heal, and constantly rebuild themselves

You might think of your skeleton as a static scaffold—dead support structure. But your bones are alive.

💡 What you'll discover:

  • You're born with about 270 bones; many fuse together, leaving adults with 206
  • Bone is five times stronger than steel of the same weight
  • Your skeleton is completely replaced every 10 years through constant remodeling
