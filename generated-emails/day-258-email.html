🌍 Earth's Different Zones

Biomes are Earth's life zones—each with distinct climate, plants, and animals

Polar bears don't live in deserts. Cacti don't grow in rainforests.

💡 What you'll discover:

  • There are roughly 10-15 major biomes on Earth, from tundra to tropical rainforest
  • Rainforests cover only 6% of Earth's surface but contain over half of all species
  • The same latitude can have very different biomes depending on ocean currents and altitude
