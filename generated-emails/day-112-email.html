☸️ The Invention of the Wheel

The wheel took thousands of years to invent—simple doesn't mean obvious

The wheel seems obvious—round things roll. But humans built cities, wrote languages, and raised pyramids for thousands of years before inventing the wheel.

💡 What you'll discover:

  • The wheel was invented around 3500 BCE—thousands of years after humans built pyramids
  • Wheels were first used for pottery, not transportation
  • The Americas never invented the wheel for transportation, despite having rolling toys

Five minutes. I think you'll love it.
