🤔 How to Think About Thinking

Metacognition is thinking about your own thinking—the skill that improves all other thinking

You can think about anything—but can you think about your thinking? This is metacognition: the ability to observe your own mental processes. It's the skill that lets you improve all other skills, because you can see where you're going wrong.

💡 What you'll discover:

  • The Dunning-Kruger effect shows that poor performers often can't recognize their own incompetence
  • Expert chess players spend more time analyzing their thinking than novices do
  • Writing forces metacognition—externalizing thoughts makes them visible for examination

Five minutes. I think you'll love it.

🤔 Today's Growth Challenge

Observe Your Mind

After making a decision or solving a problem today, write a few sentences about how you thought. What information did you use? What did you assume? Where were you uncertain? This metacognitive practice makes invisible thinking visible.
