📚 Helping Others Understand

Teaching is the art of causing understanding—and it deepens the teacher's understanding too

Want to really learn something? Teach it. The moment you try to explain, you discover what you don't actually understand.

💡 What you'll discover:

  • Students who teach material to others retain 90% compared to 10% for reading alone
  • The 'Feynman Technique' for learning: explain it like you're teaching a child, find gaps, simplify, repeat
  • Children who tutor younger children improve their own performance significantly

Five minutes. I think you'll love it.

📚 Today's Growth Challenge

Explain Something

Take something you're learning or know well. Explain it to someone—a friend, family member, or even an imaginary student. Notice where you struggle to explain clearly. Those are your knowledge gaps. Teaching is learning.
