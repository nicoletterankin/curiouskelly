🌍 Where Species Belong

Every species evolved in a place—adapted to specific conditions that shaped everything about it

Polar bears can't survive in jungles. Tropical fish die in cold water.

💡 What you'll discover:

  • Island species often evolve to be larger or smaller than their mainland relatives—the 'island rule'
  • Australia's unique mammals evolved in isolation for 45 million years
  • Invasive species cause billions of dollars in damage when introduced to ecosystems they didn't evolve in

Five minutes. I think you'll love it.
