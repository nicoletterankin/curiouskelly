🥕 What Counts as a Vegetable

Vegetable is a cooking term, not a scientific one—biology has no category called 'vegetable'

Is a tomato a fruit or a vegetable? Botanically, it's a fruit—the ripened ovary of a flower. Culinarily, it's a vegetable—we use it in savory dishes.

💡 What you'll discover:

  • Tomatoes, peppers, and cucumbers are fruits (ripened ovaries), but we call them vegetables in cooking
  • Legally, tomatoes were declared vegetables by the U.S. Supreme Court in 1893—for tax purposes
  • Botanically, a strawberry isn't a berry, but a banana is
