❓ The Power of Good Questions

The questions you ask determine the answers you find

Einstein said if he had one hour to save the world, he'd spend 55 minutes defining the problem and 5 minutes solving it. The quality of your questions determines the quality of your answers—and ultimately, the quality of your life.

💡 What you'll discover:

  • Einstein said if he had an hour to solve a problem, he'd spend 55 minutes defining the question
  • Open-ended questions activate more brain regions than yes/no questions
  • The Socratic method—teaching through questions—is over 2,400 years old and still works

Five minutes. I think you'll love it.

❓ Today's Growth Challenge

Better Inquiries

Take a problem you're facing and write down the question you've been asking about it. Then reframe it three different ways—make it broader, make it more specific, make it about possibility. Notice which reframe feels most useful.
