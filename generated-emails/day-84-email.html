👁️ How Your Eyes See

Your eyes don't see—they collect light; your brain creates the image

You think you see the world as it is. But here's the truth: your eyes collect light; your brain invents the picture.

💡 What you'll discover:

  • Your eyes have about 130 million light-detecting cells, but only 1 million nerve fibers to carry signals
  • The image on your retina is upside down—your brain flips it right-side up
  • You have a blind spot in each eye where the optic nerve connects—your brain fills it in
