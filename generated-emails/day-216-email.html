🧒 Knowledge You're Born With

Instinct is inherited wisdom—behaviors we're born knowing, without being taught

No one taught you to be startled by sudden noises. No one taught you to prefer sweet over bitter.

💡 What you'll discover:

  • Babies are born with a fear of falling and loud noises—no learning required
  • Sea turtles hatch on beaches and immediately head toward the ocean—they've never seen it before
  • The instinct to see faces is so strong that we see them in clouds, outlets, and car fronts—pareidolia

Five minutes. I think you'll love it.

🧒 Today's Growth Challenge

Ancestral or Current?

Identify a strong gut feeling you have—about risk, people, situations. Ask: is this instinct calibrated for current reality, or for ancestral conditions? Use instinct as input, not final answer. Reason can update what instinct can't.
