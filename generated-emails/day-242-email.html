⚡ How Bodies Make Energy

Metabolism converts food into usable energy—your body is a controlled combustion engine

You're burning right now. Slowly, controlled, but burning.

💡 What you'll discover:

  • Your body produces enough heat to boil half a gallon of water per hour
  • The brain uses 20% of your body's energy despite being only 2% of your weight
  • ATP—the energy currency of cells—is produced and consumed so fast that you cycle through your body weight in ATP daily
