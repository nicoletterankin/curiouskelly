🧠 How Your Brain Changes

Neuroplasticity means your brain rewires itself based on experience—you're not fixed, you're forming

Your brain isn't fixed hardware—it's constantly rewiring based on what you do. Every skill you practice, every thought you repeat, every habit you maintain—these literally reshape your brain's physical structure.

💡 What you'll discover:

  • London taxi drivers have physically larger hippocampi from memorizing the city's complex street layout
  • Learning a new skill creates new neural connections within hours
  • The brain generates new neurons throughout life—neurogenesis continues into old age
