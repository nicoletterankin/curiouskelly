🎹 Machines That Make Music

Electronic instruments expanded music beyond physical limits—creating sounds that never existed before

For thousands of years, music came from physical things—strings vibrating, air in tubes, surfaces struck. Then electricity arrived.

💡 What you'll discover:

  • The first electronic instrument, the theremin (1920), is played without touching it—using electromagnetic fields
  • The Moog synthesizer could create sounds no acoustic instrument had ever made
  • Today, a laptop can simulate entire orchestras or generate sounds never heard by human ears
