💡 Key Insights

• Regular gratitude practice improves sleep, reduces pain, and boosts immunity

• Expressing gratitude to others strengthens relationships more than receiving it

• The brain can't hold gratitude and anxiety simultaneously

🙏 Gratitude Expression - Say It Out Loud

Write down 10 things you're grateful for—be specific. Then choose one person to thank today. Send them a message, call them, or tell them in person. Notice how expressing gratitude feels.
