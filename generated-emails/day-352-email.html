🧘 Quieting Your Thoughts

Your mind thinks 60,000 thoughts a day—most are reruns

Right now, as you read this, your mind is running background processes—worries, to-dos, random memories. What if you could turn down that noise?

Today we explore the science of mental stillness—and why trying to think less usually makes you think more.

💡 What you'll discover:

  • The average person has 60,000 thoughts per day—95% are the same as yesterday
  • Just 10 minutes of meditation can reduce anxiety by 22%
  • Long-term meditators show fundamentally different brain activity patterns

🧘 Today's Growth Challenge

Mindfulness Practice

Set a timer for 3 minutes. Sit comfortably, close your eyes, and count your breaths from 1 to 10. When you lose count, start over without judgment.

Three minutes is all it takes to start rewiring how your brain handles mental chatter.
