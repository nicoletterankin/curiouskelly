💊 How Medicine Heals

Medicine works with your body—helping it do what it already knows how to do

Medicine seems like magic—swallow a pill, feel better. But most medicine isn't replacing your body's healing; it's supporting it.

💡 What you'll discover:

  • Aspirin was derived from willow bark—a folk remedy used for thousands of years before we understood why it worked
  • Antibiotics don't kill viruses—they only work on bacteria
  • Your body heals most minor injuries and illnesses on its own; medicine often just helps it along
