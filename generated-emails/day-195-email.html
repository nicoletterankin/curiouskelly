🔑 Owning What You Do

Responsibility is accepting ownership of your choices and their consequences—the price of freedom and power

Blame is tempting. It shifts weight off your shoulders.

💡 What you'll discover:

  • The word 'responsibility' literally means 'ability to respond'
  • Research shows taking responsibility increases sense of control and reduces anxiety
  • Children as young as three show desire for credit—and avoidance of blame

Five minutes. I think you'll love it.

🔑 Today's Growth Challenge

Blame to Power

Identify something you've blamed on external factors—circumstances, other people, bad luck. Ask: What part of this am I responsible for? What could I do differently? Shifting from blame to ownership doesn't add guilt; it adds power.
