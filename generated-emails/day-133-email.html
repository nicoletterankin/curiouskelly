🌾 Where Food Comes From

Every bite you eat was once alive—plants, animals, fungi—transformed into food

That sandwich on your plate? The bread was wheat growing in a field. The lettuce was leaves absorbing sunlight.

💡 What you'll discover:

  • The average meal travels over 1,500 miles from farm to plate in developed countries
  • Most of the world's food comes from just 12 plant species and 5 animal species
  • A single ear of corn has about 800 kernels—each one capable of growing a new plant

Follow the Chain

Pick one item from your next meal and trace its origin as far as you can. Where was it grown? How far did it travel? Who touched it along the way? This simple exercise reveals the invisible systems that feed you.
