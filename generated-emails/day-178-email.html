🌌 What's Beyond Earth

Space is vast beyond comprehension—distances so immense that light takes years to cross them

Look up at night and you see ancient light. Some stars you see died millions of years ago—their light is still traveling.

💡 What you'll discover:

  • Light from the sun takes 8 minutes to reach Earth—you always see the sun as it was 8 minutes ago
  • The nearest star (after our sun) is 4.24 light-years away—about 40 trillion kilometers
  • There are more stars in the observable universe than grains of sand on all Earth's beaches

Five minutes. I think you'll love it.

🌌 Today's Growth Challenge

Look Up

On a clear night, look at the stars. Choose one and imagine the light traveling for years to reach you. How does that timescale compare to your daily concerns? Cosmic perspective doesn't solve problems, but it can shift how they feel.
