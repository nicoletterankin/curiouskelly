🧬 How Life Changes Over Time

Evolution is gradual change through selection—what works survives, what doesn't disappears

Every living thing is related. Trace your ancestry far enough back and you share grandparents with every creature—fish, trees, bacteria.

💡 What you'll discover:

  • Humans share 50% of their DNA with bananas—all life evolved from common ancestors
  • Evolution continues today—bacteria evolving antibiotic resistance is evolution in real-time
  • Human brains evolved for environments that no longer exist—we're running ancient software in modern conditions
