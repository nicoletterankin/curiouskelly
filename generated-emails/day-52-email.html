🌋 What's Inside a Volcano

Volcanoes are Earth's pressure valves—releasing energy from deep below

Beneath your feet, Earth is molten. The planet's interior is so hot that rock flows like thick honey.

💡 What you'll discover:

  • Lava temperature ranges from 1,300°F to 2,200°F—hot enough to melt steel
  • There are about 1,500 active volcanoes on Earth; about 50 erupt each year
  • Volcanic eruptions have caused mass extinctions but also created fertile soil and new land
