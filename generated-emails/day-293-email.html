🌟 Natural Abilities and Practice

Talent is natural aptitude—a head start that still requires practice to develop fully

Some people seem naturally gifted—they learn faster, pick up skills easily. But even prodigies practice.

💡 What you'll discover:

  • Even Mozart practiced intensively—his early works were mostly arrangements, not original compositions
  • Studies show that by age 20, elite musicians have practiced about 10,000 hours more than average ones
  • Natural talent accounts for roughly 25% of performance differences—the rest is training
