🩸 What Blood Does All Day

Blood is your body's river—delivering oxygen, nutrients, and immune cells everywhere

Right now, a river is flowing through you. Five liters of blood, circulating through 60,000 miles of vessels, delivering oxygen to every cell, picking up waste, fighting infections, healing wounds.

💡 What you'll discover:

  • Your blood travels about 12,000 miles per day—that's nearly halfway around Earth
  • You have about 25 trillion red blood cells; your body makes 2 million new ones every second
  • A single drop of blood contains millions of cells doing different jobs

Five minutes. I think you'll love it.
