🤸 Preparing Your Body

Warm-up prepares your body for demands—transitioning from rest to performance gradually

Cold muscles tear. Cold engines stall.

💡 What you'll discover:

  • Warm muscles are more flexible and less prone to injury than cold muscles
  • A proper warm-up increases heart rate gradually, preparing the cardiovascular system
  • Mental warm-ups also exist—athletes visualize before performing

Five minutes. I think you'll love it.

Where Do You Skip Warm-Up?

Where do you jump straight into demands without warm-up—exercise, work, difficult conversations? What preparation phase could you add? Design a warm-up routine for a demanding activity in your life.
