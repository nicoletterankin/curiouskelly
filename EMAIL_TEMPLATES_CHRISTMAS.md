## 🎯 Email Best Practices

### Subject Line Rules
- ✅ Use emojis (high open rates)
- ✅ Include day number for daily lessons
- ✅ Create urgency for offers
- ✅ Personal and warm tone
- ❌ Don't use all caps
- ❌ Don't oversell

### Body Copy Rules
- ✅ Write as Kelly (first person)
- ✅ Short paragraphs (3-4 lines max)
- ✅ Clear single CTA per email
- ✅ Personal address ([Name])
- ✅ Progress visibility
- ❌ Don't be salesy
- ❌ Don't overwhelm with info

### CTA Best Practices
- ✅ One primary CTA (button)
- ✅ Action-oriented ("Start Lesson", "Explore Calendar")
- ✅ Visible and large
- ✅ Repeat at top and bottom for long emails
- ❌ Don't use multiple competing CTAs

### Tone & Voice
- **Warm:** Like a friend, not a corporation
- **Encouraging:** Celebrate progress
- **Patient:** No shame for missing days
- **Excited:** Genuinely enthusiastic about teaching
- **Personal:** Kelly talks directly to you
